TENDER NOTICE NIT-GOV-2026-010
1. SCOPE: Supply, testing, installation, and commissioning of equipment conforming to national standards for Solar Clean Energy.
2. MANDATORY SPECIFICATIONS:
   a) Primary material supply must conform to IS 14286 (latest edition).
   b) Allied items and sub-assemblies shall conform to IS 16221 and IS 16169.
   c) Workmanship, installation guidelines, and safety criteria shall strictly follow IS 17293 and IS 3043.
3. QUALITY CONTROL & INSPECTION: All bidders must submit valid BIS licenses / CRS registration certificates and NABL accredited lab test reports.
