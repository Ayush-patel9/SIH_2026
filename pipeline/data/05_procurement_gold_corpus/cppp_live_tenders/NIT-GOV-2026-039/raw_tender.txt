TENDER NOTICE NIT-GOV-2026-039
1. SCOPE: Supply, testing, installation, and commissioning of equipment conforming to national standards for IT & Telephony.
2. MANDATORY SPECIFICATIONS:
   a) Primary material supply must conform to IS 13252 (latest edition).
   b) Allied items and sub-assemblies shall conform to IS 16046 and IS 616.
   c) Workmanship, installation guidelines, and safety criteria shall strictly follow IS 16242 and IS 18250.
3. QUALITY CONTROL & INSPECTION: All bidders must submit valid BIS licenses / CRS registration certificates and NABL accredited lab test reports.
