0. FOREWORD 

0.1 This Indian Standard was adopted by the Indian Standards 
Institution on 10 October 1983, after the draft finalized by the Soil 
Working Equipment Sectional Committee had been approved by the 
Agricultural and Food Products Division Council. 

0.2 The share is an important soil engaging component of mouldboard 
plough bottom. Being soil engaging part, it wears out earlier than other 
components and has to be replaced frequently. Therefore, to ensure 
interchangeability, necessary dimensions of the shares have been speci- 
fied in this standard. 

0.3 This standard is related to the manufacturing and trade practices 
prevailing in the country. 

0.4 For the purpose of deciding whether a particular requirement of 
this standard is complied with, the final value, observed or calculated, 
expressing the result of a test, shall be rounded off in accordance with 
IS : 2-1960*. The number of significant places retained in the rounded 
off value should be the same as that of the specified value in this 
standard. 



1. SCOPE 

1.1 This standard specifies material, dimensional and other requirements 
for shares for tractor-operated mouldboard ploughs. 

2. TERMINOLOGY 

2.1 For the purpose of this standard, the definitions given in 2 of 
IS: 6327-19711 shall apply.

3. TYPES 

3.1 On the basts of the dimensions, the share shall be of the following 
7 types: 

a) Type 1, 

b) Type 2, 

c) Type 3, 

d) Type 4, 

e) Type 5, 

f) Type 6, and 

g) Type 7. 

4. MATERIAL 

4.1 The share shall be manufactured from chilled cast iron or steel con- 
forming to Grade 75C6 of IS : 1570 ( Part 2 )-1979*. Steel equivalent 
or better than this grade may also be used. The composition of Grade 
75C6 is given below for guidance: 

Carbon 0-70 to 0-80 percent 

Manganese 0-50 to 0-80 percent 

Note — The sulphur and phosphorus content shall not be more than 0*05 percent 
each. 

4.2 The material for bar point shall be 40C8 or 55C8 of IS : 5517-1978)-. 

5. HARDNESS 

5.1 The chilled cast iron shares shall have a Brinell hardness of 360 to 
400 HB when tested in accordance with IS : 1789-1961 J and depth of 
chilling shall be not less than 1'5 mm. 

5.2 The cutting edge of steel share shall be hardened and tempered to 
give a Brinell hardness of 350 to 450 HB when tested in accordance with 
IS : 1500 - 1968§.

6.1 The dimensions of types 1 to 7 plough shares shall be as given in 
Fig. 1 to 7. The tolerances on the dimensions unless indicated other- 
wise, shall be ±10 percent.

6.2 The dimensions of bar for plough share shall be in accordance with

7. OTHER REQUIREMENTS 

7.1 The cutting edge of the share shall be bevelled to a distance not 
more than 10 mm. The thickness of the cutting edge shall be between 
0-5 to 2 mm and should be uniform, as far as possible. 

7.2 The counter sunk bolts of 10 mm size shall be used for fixing the 
share with the frog. As far as possible, the bolt of M 10 size should be 
used. 

7.3 The shares shall be supplied with bolts in holes. 

8. FINISH AND WORKMANSHIP 

8.1 The shares shall be free from flaws, scratches, cracks and other de- 
fects. All fins, burrs, flashes and sharp edges other than the cutting 
edge shall be removed. 

8.2 In case of steel shares, the welding of gunnel shall be satisfactory in 
all respects. The welding shall not be porous.
