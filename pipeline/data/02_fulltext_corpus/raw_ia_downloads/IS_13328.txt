A-4 PROCEDURE 

A-4.1 Preparation of Internal Standard Solution 

"Weigh accurately 4-0 g of the internal standard 
into a 100-ml volumetric flask, dissolve and 
make up the volume with acetone. Shake well 
to homogenize. 



A-4.2 Preparation of Reference Standard Solution 

Weigh accurately 0-2 g triadimefon reference 
standard into a 100-ml volumetric flask, add to 
it 10 ml of internal standard solution and dilute 
to the mark with acetone. Shake well to 
homogenize. 

A-4.3 Preparation of Sample Solution 

Weigh accurately a quantity of sample so as to 
contain about 0-22 — 0-23 g of triadimefon into a 
100-ml volumetric flask, add to it 10 ml of 
internal standard solution and dilute to the 
mark with acetone. Shake well to homogenize. 

A-4.4 Determination 

Inject 3 fi\ reference standard solution into the 
gas chromatograph with the help of a microlitre 
syringe. From the integrator, print out and 
note the peak areas of internal standard and 
triadimefon. Similarly, inject the same volume 
of sample solution and note down the peak 
areas. Compute the percentage of triadimefon 
content in the sample.
