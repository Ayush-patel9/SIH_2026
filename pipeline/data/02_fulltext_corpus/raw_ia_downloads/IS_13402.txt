A-2.4.1 Preparation of Internal Standard Solution

A-2. 4. 2 Preparation of Reference Standard Solution

system. Again take out 10 ml of this solution 
into another 50-ml volumetric flask, add 10 ml 
internal standard solution, make up the volume 
to the mark with the solvent system. 

A-2. 4.3 Preparation of Sample Solution 

Warm up about 100-150 g anilophos, technical 
sample in a container, on a water-bath maintained

mass into a beaker with a previously weighed 
glass-rod. Allow the beaker and the contents to 
cool at room temperature and weigh till a 
constant weight is obtained. Dissolve the simple 
in toluene and quantitatively transfer with care 
into a 100-ml volumetric flask. Wash the beaker 
several times with toluene, adding the washings 
to the flask. Make up the volume with toluene 
and shake well. Takeout 10 ml of the solution 
with a pipette into a 50-ml volumetric (lask and

(see A-2.3.1). Mix well. Again take out 10 ml 
of this solution into another 50-ml volumetric

make up the volume to the mark with the solvent 
system.
