Bitertanol content is estimated on a gas chrom-

Dioctyl phthalate is used as the internal 
standard.

A-4 PROCEDURE 

A-4.1 Preparation of Internal Standard Solution 

Weigh accurately 2*4 g of the internal standard 
into a 50-ml volumetric flask, dissolve and 
make up the volume with acetone. Shake well 
to homogenize. 



A-4.2 Preparation of Reference Standard Solution 

Weigh accurately 0*3 g bitertanol reference 
standard into a 50-ml volumetric flask, add to 
it 5 ml of internal standard solution and dilute 
to the mark with acetone. Shake well to 
homogenize. 

A-4.3 Preparation of Sample Solution 

Weigh accurately a quantity of sample so as 
to contain about 0-3 g of bitertanol into a 
50-ml volumetric flask, add to it 5 ml of internal 
standard solution and dilute to the mark with 
acetone. Shake well to homogenize. 

A-4.4 Determination 

Inject 2 fx\ reference standard solution into the 
gas chromatograph with the help of a microlitre 
syringe. From the integrator, print out and 
note the peak areas of internal standard and 
bitertanol. Similarly, inject the same volume 
of sample solution and note down the peak 
areas. Compute the percentage of bitertanol 
content in the sample.
