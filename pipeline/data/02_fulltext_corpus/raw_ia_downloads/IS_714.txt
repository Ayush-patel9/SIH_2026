FOREWORD 

This Indian Standard ( Third Revision ) was adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards, after the 
draft finalized by the Textile Materials for Aerospace Purposes Sectional Committee had been 
approved by the Textile Division Council. 

The second revision of this standard was published in 1979, and it has been again revised on the basis 
of experience gained during its use. The following are the main changes carried out in ^his version: 

a) Conditions of the tapes, in which it is to be supplied have been included. 

b) Reference has been made to IS 11662 : 1986 for the purpose of carrying out rot-proofness. 

c) Requirement for colour fastness has been included. 

For the purpose of deciding whether a particular requirement of this standard is complied with, the 
final value, observed or calculated, expressing the result of a test or analysis, shall be rounded off in 
accordance with IS 2 : 1960 *Rules for rounding off numerical values ( revised)\ The number of signi- 
ficant places retained in the rounded off value should be the same as that of the specified value iri this 
standard.

3 MANUFACTURE 
3.1 Yarn 

The tapes shall be manufactured from cotton 
^arns with z/s/z twist in the warp and z/s twist 



in the weft. The nominal count of warp and 
weft yarns shall be as follows: 

Warp 30 texx 3 X 4 ( 20s/3/4Ne ) 
Weft 25texx2(24s/2Ne) 

3.1.1 The yarn shall be evenly spun and free 
from spinning and doubling defects. 

3.2 The tapes shall be uniform in construction 
and with firm, straight and even selvedges. 

3.2.1 The tapes shall also be free from sizing, 
filling, dressing materials and substances liable 
to cause subsequent tendering. 

3.3 Finish 

3.3.1 General 

The tape shall be supplied in one of the follow- 
ing conditions, as specified in the contract or 
order: 

a) Scoured; 

b) Scoured and rot-proofed; and 

c) Scoured, dyed, rot-proofed. 

3.3.2 Dyeing 

If dyeing is required, the tape or the yarns from 
which it is to be constructed shall be dyed in 
colour as specified in the contract or order. 
The sulphur dyes shall not be used. 

3.3.3 Rot-Proofing 

If rot-proofing is required, the tape shall be- 
treated with pentachlorophenyl laurate (PCPL ) 
in accordance with IS 11662: 1986 using the 
normal process.

4.1 The tape shall conform to the physical 
requirements specified in Table 1. 

4.2 The tape shall also conform to the chemical 
requirements specified in Table 2.
