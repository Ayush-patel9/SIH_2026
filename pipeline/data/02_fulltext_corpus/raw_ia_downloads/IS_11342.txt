This Indian Standard (First Revision) was adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards, after the draft 
finalized by the Plastics Containers Sectional Committee had been approved by the Petroleum, Coal 
and Related Products Division Council. 

Sacks made from thermoplastic films are finding their widespread use in the packing of bulk materials 
in grannular and dry powder form. Differences in the method of measurements and expression of 
the dimensions of sacks made from thermoplastic films occur both nationally and internationally. 



This standard was first published in 1 985 and was based on the Draft Internationa! Standard ISO/DIS 
6591/2 'Packing sacks— Description and method of measurement : Part 2 Sacks made from 
thermoplastics flexible film' issued by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO). The 
object of this standard was to guide the purchasers and manufacturers of these sacks in specifying 
these sacks and overcome the differences in the expression and measurement of the specified 
dimension to their mutual benefit. The present revision of the standard has been taken up as a result 
of review of the standard by the concerned technical commiilee and it has been decided to adopt 
ISO 6591/2 - 1985 Packaging — Sacks — Description and method of measurement — Part 2 : 
Empty sacks made from thermoplastic flexible film*, issued by International Organization for Stand- 
ardization (ISO), under dual numbering system.

5 Method of measurement 

5.1 Equipment 

Ruler or instrument, graduated in millimetres, and capable of 
measuring dimensions of sack to an accuracy of 1 mm, 

5.2 Sampling 

Sampling shall be carried out in accordance with ISO 7023 by 
taking at least 10 sample sacks. 

5.3 Procedure 

Place the sack on flat horizontal surface, and smooth out any 
wrinkles. Measure the dimension at the measuring points in- 
dicated in clause 4 for the type of sack concerned. Measure at 
least 10 sacks to an accuracy of not less than 1 mm. 

5.4 Catcutation and expression of results 

Calculate the dimensions as the mean of the determinations, 
and express them in millimetres to the nearest millimetre.
