This Indian Standard was adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards, after the draft finalized by 
the Transport Tractors and Trailers Sectional Committee had been approved by the Transport 
Engineering Division Council.

code which would lay down standards to assess suitability of trailer in its intended role.

3 TERMINOLOGY 

3.1 For the purpose of this standard, the terms 
and definitions given in IS 7774 ( Parts 1 and 2 ) : 
1975 and along with the following shall apply. 

3.1.1 Articulation Height for Single Axle 

It is the maximum lift of wheels on one end of 
the axle to the other end of the same tilted axle 
without affecting the ease of operation/functions 
of the various components/controls. 

3.1.2 Articulation Height for Multi Axle 

It is the maximum height to which any two 
adjacent diagonally opposite wheels of an axle 
group of any trailer be subjected to lift without 
affecting the ease of operation/functions of 
various components/controls. 

3.1.3 Near Side 

Near side of a tractor-trailer combination is the 
side which is nearer to the platform kerb or 
pavement where a tractor-trailer combination 
is usually parked for short periods on 
highways in the direction of move. The near 
side is the one towards the left of the driver when 
he is in the driving seat. 

3.1.4 Offside 

Off side of a trailer is the side opposite to the 
near side.

4 PERFORMANCE TESTS 

4.1 General Performance Tests 

4.1.1 Initial Examination 

Any new trailer on receipt from the firm shall 
be inspected to ascertain whether the tractor- 
trailer combination is in conformity with the 
specifications and drawings of the approved 
prototype supplied by the makers. 

4.1.2 Loading and Weighing 

The sample trailer shall be weighed in laden and 
unladen conditions to check axle load distribu- 
tions. In laden condition, the trailers shall be 
loaded with the specified load and locations as per 
the specification. 

4.1.3 Road Test on Selected Track on Public Road 

4.1.3.1 A suitable test track consisting of different 
types of road surfaces like tar, tarmac and gravel, 
representing the general public road/high way 
conditions shall be selected for this test. 



4.1.3.2 The track shall be of at least 15 km run. 

4.1.3.3 The towing vehicle with the trailer in 
combination shall be driven in unladen/laden con- 
dition at specified restricted speed and maximum 
controllable speed. 

4.1.3.4 General performance of the trailer during 
this test shall be recorded. 



4.1.4 Articulation Test 

4.1.4.1 The test shall 
levelled hard ground. 



be conducted on a plain 



4.1.4.2 The test shall be conducted in the fully 
laden condition of the trailer with specified tyre 
pressure. 

4.1.4.3 Articulation height of each of the con- 
secutive pairs of wheels shall be measured. 

4.1.4.4 Observations regarding the fouling points, 
if any, shall be recorded.

4.1.5 Gradeability 

The gradeability of a tractor-trailer combination 
is checked in fully laden condition. When prime 
mover is detached, the trailer shall be able to 
hold with parking brake at the specified gradient. 

4.1.6 Measurement of Turning Circle 

4.1. 6.1 Turning circle diameter of the tractor- 
trailer combination shall be measured on a plain 
ground. 

4.1.6.2 Marking can be made on the ground, 
while the tractor-trailer combination is moved in 
the lowest possible speed at different types of 
drive, either by means of spray water or by means 
of a pointer. 

4.1.6.3 Both minimum outer and corresponding 
inner circle shall be checked. 

4.1.6.4 Minimum outer tractor-trailer combina- 
tion clearance circle and the corresponding inner 
tractor-trailer combination clearance circle shall 
be checked. 



4.1.7 Brake Performance Tests 

The brake performance shall be checked in 
accordance with IS 1 1852 ( Part 3 ) : 1987. 

4.1.8 Lighting Equipment Tests 

All the electrical fittings like tractor-trailer 
couplers, brake lamps, indicator lamps, tail lamps, 
etc, shall be checked during day as well as night 
for intended functions. 



4.2 Optional Performance Tests 

4.2.1 Road Tests on Cross Country Track 

4.2.1.1 The test track selected for this test shall 
be of rough, uneven boulder and hilly terrain. 

4.2.1.2 It is preferable if the track includes 
crossing of river beds and slushy terrain and shall 
be of at least 15 km run. 

4.2.1.3 General performance of the trailer shall 
be recorded during this test. 

4.2.2 Endurance Trials 

4.2.2.1 After a trailer has been tested for assess- 
ment of its performance under various tests/trials 
as explained above, it may be subjected to 
endurance trials. The aim of these trials is to 
observe the sturdiness and capability of the 
trailers, its components assemblies and parts and 
to record any defects, maintenance diflScuIties 
etc, that would be normally experienced during 
prolonged service under actual operating condi- 
tions and which could not be brought out during 
standard performance trials. 

4.2.2.2 The trailer, which is selected for endurance 
trials shall be kept under observation during the 
trial period. The required kilometerage for 
completion of endurance trials shall depend opon 
the type of trailer and its intended role. During 
endurance trials, the trailers shall be used in their 
intended role without any misuse or abuse of any 
part or any assembly/component. The trailer 
shall be run in highway/cross country/kacha 
roads as recommended for the trailer use. 

4.2.2.3 After completion of endurance trials, 
various major assemblies/sub-assemblies shall be 
stripped open to check the condition of various 
components and wear rates of various moving 
parts.
