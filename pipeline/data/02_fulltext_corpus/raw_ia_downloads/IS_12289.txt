0. FOREWORD 



0.1 This Indian Standard was adopted by the 
Bureau of Indian Standards on 25 January 1988, 
after the draft finalized by the Electrical 
Appliances Sectional Committee had been 
approved by the Electrotechnical Division 
Council. 

0.2 This standard covers the general, safety and 
performance requirements of hand held electric 
engraving tools. 

0.3 This standard is to be read in conjunction 
with IS : 302-1979*. For the sake of con- 
venience, the clauses of this standard corres- 
pond to those of IS : 302-1979. Instead of 
reproducing full text of each clause, clauses of

'Not applicable'.

be replaced by a new text, it has been indicated 
as under:

text'. 

Any addition to the existing provisions of a 
subclause of IS : 302-1979 has been indicated as 
under: 



'Addition — Followed by the text of the 
additional matter'.

of IS : 302-1979, are numbered starting from 101

18. ENDURANCE 

This clause of IS : 302-1979* is applicable 
except as follows: 

18.1 Replacement 

Appliances shall be so constructed that, in 
normal use, there will be no electrical or mecha- 
nical failure that might impair compliance with 
this standard. The insulation shall not be 
damaged and contacts and connections shall 
not work loose as a result of heating, vibration, 
etc. 

Moreover, overload protection devices shall 
not operate under normal running conditions. 

18.2 Replacement 

The tool is operated intermittently with no 
load for 1 000 cycles at a voltage equal to I'l 
times rated voltage and then for 1 000 cycles at a 
supply voltage equal to 0*9 times rated voltage. 
Each cycle of operation comprises an *on' period 
of 5 min and an 'off' period of 10 min. 

The operating period for tools for short-time 
or intermittent operation is equal to the operat- 
ing time. 

If the temperature rise of any part of the 
tool exceeds the temperature rise determined 
during the test of 11.1, forced coohng or rest 
periods are applied, the rest periods being 
excluded from the specified operating time. 

During these tests, overload protection devi- 
ces shall not operate. 

18.3 and 18.4 — Not applicable. 
18.5 Replacement 

After the tests of 18.2, the appliance shall 
withstand the tests of 16, the limits of insultation 
resistance being, however, reduced by 50 
percent. 

Connections, handles, guards and other 
fittings or components shall not have worked 
loose and there shall be no deterioration impair- 
ing safety in normal use.

or the like incorporated into the apphance may 
be used to provide the necessary protection.

for the same appliance, these tests are made 
consecutively.

Additional Clauses 

101. PERFORMANCE TEST 

101.1 The engraving tool shall be operated at 
the rated voltage and engraving shall be done 
on the surface for which it is recommended to 
be suitable by the manufacturer. During this 
operation, the hand shall exert normal pressure 
on the tool. 

The engravings obtained shall be smooth and 
easily legible. It shall not be possible to rub off 
the same with the help of a piece of cloth. 

102. TESTS 

102.0 Categories of Tests — Tests are classified 
as type, acceptance and routine tests. 

102.1 Type Tests — The tests specified in 
Table 101 shall constitute type tests and shall be 
carried out on two samples of the same type and 
rating selected preferably at random from a 
regular production lot. Before commencement 
of tests, the engraving tools shall be visually 
examined and inspected for obvious visual 
defects in respect of components, parts and their 
assembly, construction, stability, markings, 
provision of suitable terminals for supply connec- 
tions, earthing, and the effectiveness of screws 
and connections. The external surface finish 
shall be even and free from finishing defects. 

102,1.1 Both the samples shall successfully 
pass all the type tests of proving conformity 
with requirements of the standard. If any of 
the samples should fail in any of the type test(s), 
the testing authority at its discretion may call 
for fresh samples not exceeding twice the origi- 
nal number and subject them again to all the 
tests or to the test(s) in which failure(s) occurred. 
No failure shall be permitted in the repeat tests.

constitute the acceptance tests:
