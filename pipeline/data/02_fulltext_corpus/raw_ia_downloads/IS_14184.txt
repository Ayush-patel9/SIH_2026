The  product  shall  consist  of  dry,  free  flowing

the  amount  specified  in  3.2.3.

than  5  percent  by  mass  of  the  product  shall  pass

method  described  in  12.1  of  IS  6940  :  1982. 

3.2.2,1  The  ratio  of  lower  to  upper  particle  size 
shall  not  exceed  1  :  3. 

3.2.3  Dust

in  12.1  of  IS  6940 :  1982,  not  more  than 
10  percent  by  mass  of  the  product  shall  pass 
through  75  micron  IS  sieve. 

3.2.3.1  The  portion  retained  on  the  sieve 
(  see  3.2.3  )  shall  contain  not  less  than  8  percent 
by  mass  of  the  declared  nomical  cartap  hydro- 
chloride content  when  determined  by  the 
method  described  in  Annex  A  of  IS  14159  :'  1994.

4  of  IS  6940  :  1982,  the  moisture  content  of  the 
material  shall  not  exceed  4-0  percent  by  mass.

requirements  specified  in  3.3.1  and  3.3.2.
