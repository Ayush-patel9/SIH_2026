0. FOREWORD 

0,1 This Indian Standard was adopted by the Indian Standards Institution 
on 11 December 1970, after the draft finalized by the Shipbuilding 
Sectional Committee had been approved by the Mechanical Engineering 
Division Council. 

0.2 Smoke protecting flap valves are provided in the ventilation systems on 
board ships so as to ehminate spread of fire hazards from one compartment 
to the other. 

0.3 Smoke protecting flap valves are required to be fitted in mechanical 
ventilation systems on board ships under the statutory regulations. When 
local control of smoke protecting flap valves are required to be operated 
from both sides of a bulkhead or division, flap valves are fitted on either 
side of the bulkhead with provision for local control. 

0.4 For the purpose of deciding whether a particular requirement of this 
standard is complied with, the final value, observed or calculated, 
expressing the result of a test, shall be rounded off in accordance with 
IS : 2-1960*. The number of significant places retained in the rounded off 
value should be the same as that of the specified value in this standard.

6. DESIGNATION 

6A Smoke protecting flap valves shall be designated by the title, and 
the dimension dk for circular flap valves or the dimensions Ik and bk 
for rectangular or square flap valves together with the number of this 
standard.

8. WORKMANSHIP 

8.1 The workmanship shall be of the highest order and conforming to the

9.1 The smoke protecting flap valve shall be tested for proper operation, 

9.2 A chalk test shall be applied and the circumference of the flap 
shall show a minimum of 90 percent contact for acceptance.
