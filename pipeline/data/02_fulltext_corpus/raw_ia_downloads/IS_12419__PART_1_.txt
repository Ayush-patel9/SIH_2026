according to this standard, shall be designated 
by: 

a) reference to this Indian Standard, 

b) the letters IS representing Indian Standard, 

c) A two-digit numerical block to identify 
various families of connectors.

01 to 30, 

SMB family in the block No. 
31 to 60, 

SMC family in the block No. 
61 to 90, and 

block No. 91 to 99 reserved 

as spares.

ing from 01 to 99 for each type of connec- 
tor to represent variants like materials, 
finishes, cables, cable terminating technique 
applications, etc. This block is further
