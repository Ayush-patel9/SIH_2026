be used. 

6. Testing — Each assembled valve shall be subjected to a pneumatic test at a pressure not less than 
the normal working pressure in both open and crosed positions. 

7. Safety Requirements — Ail valve spindles relying for their retention under pressure on a suitable 
thread shall have, in addition, a second means of retention to safeguard against the spindle being 
ejected with hi^h velocity thereby endangering life in the event of the failure of the engaging threads.
