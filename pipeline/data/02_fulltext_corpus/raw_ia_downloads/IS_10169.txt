0.     FOREWORD 

0.1  This  Indian  Standard  was  adopted  by  the  Indian  Standards 
Institution  on  24  May  1982,  after  the  draft  finalized  by  the  Pesticides 
Residue  Analysis  Sectional  Committee  had  been  anproved  by  the 
Agricultural  and  Food  Products  Division  Council. 

0.2  Carbaryl  formulations  are  extensively  used  in  agriculture  for  the 
control  of  many  insect  pests  of  fruits,  vegetables  and  other  crops. 
Frequent  and  increased  use  of  carbaryl  formulations  often  result  in 
harmful  effects  due  to  the  toxic  nature  of  residues.  Careful  assessment 
of  residues  is,  therefore,  an  important  step  in  safeguarding  human 
health  and  in  the  establishment  of  sound  regulatory  policy. 

0.3  In  the  preparation  of  this  standard  due  consideration  has  been  given 
to  the  limits  of  carbaryal  residues  which  have  been  laid  down  under  the 
provisions  of  Prevention  of  Food  Adulteration  Rules  and  the  specified  test 
methods  are  sensitive  to  the  prescribed  levels  of  residues. 

0.4  This  standard  will  enable  the  heahh  authorities  and  others  engaged 
in  field  to  follow  uniform  test  procedure  for  the  estimation  of  carbaryl 
residues  in  fruits  and  vegetables. 

0.5  In  reporting  the  result  of  a  test  or  analysis  made  in  accordance  with 
this  standard,  if  the  final  value,  observed  or  calculated,  is  to  be  rounded 
off,  it  shall  be  done  in  accordance  with  IS  :  2-1962*. 


1.  SCOPE 

1.1  This  standard  prescribes  spectrophotometric  and  gas  chromatogra- 
phic methods  for  the  determination  of  carbaryl  (  l-naphthyl-TV  methyl 
carbamate  )  residues  in  fruits  and  vegetables.
