PESTICIDE — PIRIMIPHOS-METHYL, 
TECHNICAL — SPECIFICATION 



1 SCOPE 

This standard prescribes the requirements and the 
methods of sampling and test for pirimiphos- 
methyl, technical. 

2 REFERENCES 

The Indian Standards listed in Annex A are 
necessary adjuncts to this standard. 

3 REQUIREMENTS 

3.1 Description 

The material shall be in the form a dark straw 
coloured to red/brown liquid at temperatures 
above 20°C. The material may contain crystals 
of pirimiphosmethyl in traces and shall be free 
from extraneous matter. 

3.2 Miscibility with Trimethyl Benzene, Technical 

One part of the material shall be completely 
miscible with three parts of trimethyl benzene, 
technical, showing not more than a faint turbidity. 

3.3 The material shall also comply with the 
requirements specified in Table 1. 

4 PACKING 

The material shall be packed in clean and dry 
mild steel drums suitably lacquered from inside. 
The container shall also comply with general 
requirements as given in IS 8190 (Part 2) : 1988. 

5 MARKING 

5.1 The container shall be securely closed and 
bear legibly and indelibly the following inform- 
ation in addition to any other information as 
required under the Insecticides Act, 1968 and 
Rules framed thereunder: 

a) Name of the material; 



b) Indication of the source of manufacturer; 

c) Date of manufacture and date of expiry; 

d) Batch number; 

e) Net mass of contents; 

f) Pirimiphos-methyl content, percent ( mint ); 
and 

g) The minimum cautionary notice as worded 
under the Insecticides Act, 1968 and Rules 
framed thereunder. 

5.2 The container may also be marked with the 
Standard Mark. 

6 SAMPLING 

Representative samples of the material shall be 
drawn as prescribed in IS 10946 : 1984. 

NOTES 

1 Before drawing representative samples, drums shall 
be checked for the presence of crystals and if found, 
must be thawed. To facilitate this, the drums may be 
stored in a warm room or partially immersed in 
moderately hot water ( maximum temperature 50 a C ) 
for a period of not more than 24 hours prior to 
sampling. 

2 Once received in the laboratory, the samples shall 
be stored at a temperature below 25°C. 

7 TESTS 

7.1 Tests shall be carried out by the methods 
referred to in 3.2 and col 4, 5 & 6 of Table 1 . 

7.2 Quality of Reagents 

Unless specified otherwise, pure chemicals and 
distilled water ( see IS 1070: 1977) shall be 
employed in tests. 

NOTE — 'Pure chemicals' shall mean chemicals that 
do not contain impurities which affect the results of 
analysis.
