FOREWORD 

This  Indian  Standard  (First  Revision)  was  adopted  by  the  Bureau  of  Indian  Standards,  after  the  draft 
finalized  by  the  Cordage  Sectional  Committee  had  been  approved  by  the  Textile  Division  Council. 

This  standard  originally  published  in  1984  has  been  revised  to  align  it  with  ISO  1141  ;  1990  'Ropes  — 
Polyester  —  Specification',  issued  by  the  International  Organization  for  Standardization.  Following 
changes  have  been  incorporated  in  the  revised  version: 

i)  Reference  number  corresponding  to  nominal  diameter  in  mm  has  been  given  and  is  meant  for 
used  as  designation  of  the  rope, 

ii)  Values  of  minimum  breaking  load  have  been  modified,  and 

iii)  Tolerance  on  linear  density  has  also  been  modified. 

For  the  purpose  of  deciding  whether  a  particular  requirement  of  this  standard  is  complied  with,  the  final 
value,  observed  or  calculated,  expressing  the  result  of  a  test  or  analysis,  shall  be  rounded  off  in  accordance 
with. IS  2  :  1960  *Riiles  for  rounding  of  numerical  values  {revised)\  The  number  of  significant  places 
retained  in  the  rounded  off  value  should  be  the  same  as  that  of  the  specified  value  in  this  standard.

1  SCOPE 

This  standard  specifies  requirements  for  the  fol- 
lowing types  of  ropes  made  out  of  polyester  mul- 
tifilament yarn: 

—  3-strand  hawser-laid 

—  8-strand  plaited 

2  REFERENCES 

The  Indian  Standards  listed  in  Annex  A  are  neces- 
sary adjuncts  to  this  standard. 

3  TERMINOLOGY 

For  the  purpose  of  this  standard,  the  definitions 
given  in  IS  3871 :  1984  shall  apply. 

4  ATMOSPHERIC  CONDITIONS  FOR 
CONDITIONING  AND  TESTS 

The  tests  may  be  carried  out  under  prevailing  at- 
mospheric conditions.  However,  in  case  of  dispute, 
the  tests  shall  be  carried  out  on  samples  which  have 
been  conditioned  for  24  hours  in  a  standard  atmos- 
pheric conditions  (65  ±  2  percent  relative  humidity 
and  27  ±  T*C  temperature).  If  practicable,  the  tests 
shall  be  carried  out  under  standard  atmospheric 
conditions.  Otherwise  the  tests  shall  be  carried  out 
as  quickly  as  possible  and  not  later  than  15  minutes 
of  removal  of  the  test  pieces  from  the  conditioning 
atmosphere. 

5  MANUFACTURE 
5.1  Construction 

5.1.1  Unless  otherwise  specified,  hawser-laid 
ropes  shall  have  *Z'  twist,  their  strands  *S'  twist  and 
their  roping  yarns  *Z*  twist. 

5.1.2  Unless  otherwise  specified,  8-strand  plaited 
ropes  shall  consist  of  four  pairs  of  strands,  each 
alternate  pair  consisting  of  two  *S*  twisted  and  two 
*Z*  twisted  strands. 


5.1.3  The  number  of  rope  yarns  in  all  the  strands 
shall  be  same. 

5.2  Structure 

The  ropes  and  their  strands  shall  be  continuous, 
without  splice. 

5.3  Treatment 

The  ropes  shall  be  heat  stabilized  to  fix  the  lay  and 
dimensional  stability.  This  treatment  shall  ensure 
dimensional  stability  so  that  subsequent  shrinkage 
on  use  shall  be  minimal. 

5.4  Rope 

The  twisted  rope  shall  be  flexible,  well  laid  and  free 
from  defects  in  yarn,  strand  and  finish. 

6  REQUIREMENTS 

6.1  Length  of  Coil 

The  length  of  each  coil  when  tested  by  the  method 
prescribed  in  IS  707 1  (Part  2) :  1989  shall  not  be  less 
than  220  m  or  as  declared.  However,  if  so  agreed 
between  the  buyer  and  the  seller,  the  length  of  coil 
may  be  tested  under  zero  tension  and  the  following 
tolerances  shall  be  applicable  on  the  specified/ 
declared  length : 
Reference  Number  Tolerance 

Up  to  14  ±5  percent 

Above  14  ±3  percent 

NOTE  —  Any  coil  which  has  been  shortened  by  cutting  from 
it,  the  necessary  test  sample  shall  be  accepted  at  its  original 
length  and  mass  as  part  of  the  delivery  provided  that  the  test 
sample  complies  with  the  specified  requirements. 

6.2  Mass  of  the  Coil 

The  net  mass  of  the  coil  containing  220  metres  of 
rope  including  ties  and  lashing  but  excluding 
packing  materials  shall  be  as  specified  in  Table  1 
and  Table  2  for  3-strand  hawser-laid  and  8-strand 
plaited  ropes  respectively.

7  PACKING 

The  ropes  shall  be  neatly  coiled  and  suitably  packed 
to  prevent  damage  during  transit. 

NOTE  —  IS  3256  :  1980  may  be  followed  for  packing  ropes 
intended  for  use  within  the  country.

8.1  Each  coil  shall  have  securely  attached  lables  at 
both  ends  carrying  the  following  information  : 


a)  Name  and  type  of  the  material, 

b)  Reference  number, 

c)  Length  of  the  rope, 

d)  Indication  of  the  source  of  manufacture,  and 

e)  Any  other  information  required  by  the 
buyer.

9  SAMPLING 

9.1  Lot 

The  quantity  of  coils  of  rope  of  the  same  linear 
density,  and  type,  manufactured  under  similar  con- 
ditions and  delivered  to  a  buyer  against  a  despatch 
note  shall  constitute  a  lot. 

9.2  Sample  Size 

Draw  the  samples  at  random,  at  the  rate  shown  by 
the  following  formula  to  ensure  that  the  samples 
are  as  representative  as  possible  of  the  lot : 

Where  5  is  the  number  of  coils  in  the  sample  and  N 
is  the  size  of  the  lot  expressed  as  the  number  of 
coils.  When  S  as  calculated  is  not  a  whole  number, 
round  off  the  value  obtained  to  give  the  whole 
number  in  accordance  with  the  requirements  of 
IS  2  :  1960.  In  cases  where  5  is  less  than  1,  draw  one 
coil  as  sample. 


9.3  For  evaluating  the  length,  linear  density, 
breaking  load  and  pitch,  the  number  of  coils 
selected  according  to  9.2  shall  constitute  the  test 
sample. 

9.3.1  For  evaluating  the  net  mass  of  the  lot,  all  the 
coils  in  the  lot  shall  constitute  the  test  specimen. 

9.4  Criteria  of  Conformity 

The  lot  should  be  declared  conforming  to  the 
standard  if  the  following  conditions  are  satisfied: 

a)  Length  of  each  coil  satisfies  the  specified/ 
declared  length; 

b)  All  the  individual  test  samples  tested  for 
breaking  load  satisfy  the  specified  require- 
ment. However,  in  case  of  failure  of  a  test 
specimen  drawn  from  a  coil,  another 
specimen  shall  be  tested  from  the  same  coil 
and  the  same  shall  satisfy  the  specified 
requirement;  and 

c)  Average  values  of  the  test  results  of  the  lot 
in  respect  of  other  characteristics  conform 
to  the  specified  requirements.
