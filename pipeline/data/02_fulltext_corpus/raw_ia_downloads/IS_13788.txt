DETERMINATION OF KITAZIN CONTENT 



A-l PRINCIPLE 



The sample is diluted to appropriate volume 
with chloroform and content is determined 
using dibutyl phthalate or equivalent as internal 
standard, by gas chromatography. 

A-2 APPARATUS 

A-2.1 Microlitre Syringe — lO/il capacity 

A-2.2 Gas Chromatograph 

The instrument shall be equipped with a flame 
ionization detector and may be coupled to a 
printer-plotter cum integrator. The operative 
parameters are suggested below, which can be 
changed in any other equipment, provided 
standardization is done: 

Column — Glass, 360 cm length X 2 mm ID; 
packed with 5 percent OV-1 on Gas 
Chrom Q ( 60-80 mesh ) 

Temperatures 

Column oven — 240°C 

Injection port — 275°C 

Detector — 300°C 
Carrier Gas and and Flow Rate 
Nitrogen ( N 2 ) — 40 ml per minute 

A-3 REAGENTS 

A-3.1 Chloroform 

A-3.2 Internal Standard — dibutyl phthalate 

A-3.3 Kitazin Reference Standard — of known 
purity 

A-4 PROCEDURE 

A-4.1 Preparation of Internal Standard Solution 

Weigh accurately 0*1 g dibutyl phthalate in a 
100-ml volumetric flask and make up the 
volume to the mark with chloroform. This 
will give a solution containing 1 mg/ml of 
dibutyl phthalate. The solution will remain 
stable for 4 weeks if it is stored in a tightly 
sealed bottle under refrigeration. Allow the 
solution to attain room temperature before use. 



A-4.2 Preparation of Standard Solution 

Weigh accurately 200 mg of kitazin reference 
standard in a 100-ml volumetric flask and make 
up the volume to the mark with chloroform. 
Take 5 ml of this solution into a 25-ml volu- 
metric flask and add 5 ml of the internal 
standard solution and finally make up the 
volume to the mark with chloroform. 

A-4.3 Preparation of Sample Solution 

Weigh accurately a quantity of the sample 
equivalent to 200 mg of kitazin in a 100-ml 
volumetric flask and make up the volume to 
to mark with chloroform. Take 5 ml of this 
solution into a 25-ml volumetric flask and add 
5 ml of the internal standard solution and finally 
make up the volume to the mark with 
chloroform. 

A-4.4 Estimation 

Inject a known volume of the standard solution 
and the sample solution with the help of a 
microlitre syringe. Measure the peak areas of 
the internal standard, standard and sample.
