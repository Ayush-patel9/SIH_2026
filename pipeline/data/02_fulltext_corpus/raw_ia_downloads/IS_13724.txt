1 Scope 

This International Standard specifies the characteristics of hexagon thin nuts {chamfered), with metric fine pitch 
thread, with nominal thread diameters d from 8 mm up to and including 64 mm, with product grade A for sizes d up 
to and including 16 mm and product grade B for sizes f/over 16 mm. 

If, in special cases, specifications other than those listed in this International Standard are required, they should be 
selected from existing International Standards, for example ISO 724, ISO 898-6, ISO 965-1, ISO 3506-2 and 
ISO 4759-1. 

Coarse thread hexagon nuts according to ISO 4035 should be the first choice.
