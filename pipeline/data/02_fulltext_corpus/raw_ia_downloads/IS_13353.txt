FOREWORD 

This Indian Standard was adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards, after the draft finalized 
by the Automotive Accessories and Garage Equipment Sectional Committee had been approved 
by the Transport Engineering Division Council. 

In the preparation of this standard, considerable assistance has been drawn from GOST 4.112— 
1984 'System of quality characteristics of products; garage equipments nomenclature of 
characteristics' issued by USSR State Committee for Standards ( GOST ).

This standard gives technical parameters of 
basic types of garage equipments.
