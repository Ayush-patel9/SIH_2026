0. FOREWORD 

0.1 This Indian Standard ( Revised ) was adopted by the Indian Standards 
Institution on 30 May 1966, after the draft finalized by the Electrical Ap- 
pliances Sectional Committee had been approved by the Electiotcchnical 
Division Council. 

0.2 This standard covers portable hand-lamps ( also known as Inspection 
lamps) with a body and handle incorporating lampholder, guard and 
strap, hook or other devices, but not including the lamps. Thi$ standard 
does not cover special types of hand-lamps, such as gastight, flameproof, 
watertight, or dustproof hand-lamps, as well as portable lamp stands and 
brackets. 

0.3 This standard covers the general, safety and performance requirements 
of electric hand-lamps to ensure reliable operation, personal safety against 
electric shock and safety against the effects of excessive temperature and 
fire. 

0.4 This standard was originally published in 1959. It has been revised 
with a view to: 

a) aligning it with IS : 302-1973*; and 

b) specifying additional requirements and test procedures, for ex- 
ample, requirements and tests for temperature limit, creepage 
distances and clearances, screws and connections, fire resisting 
properties, etc, and details on sampling procedure for accep- 
tance. 

0.4.1 The special requirements of tropical countries like India as well 
as the testing facilities at the disposal of light electrical appliances industry 
in the country have been taken into consideration while preparing this 
revision. 

0.5 IS : 302-1973* to which references have been made in this standard 
with regard to general and safety requirements as well as methods of tests 
is a necessary adjunct to this standard. Should, however, any deviation 
exist between the requirements of IS : 302-1973* and those of this standard, 
provisions of latter shall apply.

1. SCOPE 

1.1 This standard covers the requirements and methods of tests for electric 
portable hand-lamps for use on supply voltages not exceeding 250 volts 
single phase ac or dc, and suitable for use with lamps having wattages 
not exceeding 150 W. 

1.1.1 Special types of hand-lamps like flameproof, gastight, watertight 
or dustproof hand-lamps are excluded from the scope of this standard. 

1.1.2 This standard does not also cover portable lampstands and 
brackets. 

2. TERMINOLOGY 

2.0 For the purpose of this standard, the definition given in 2.1 shall apply 
in addition to those specified in 2 of IS : 302-1973t. 

2.1 Hand-Lamp — A portable electric-light fitting suitable for carrying in 
hand. It consists of a body, handle, a lampholder (excluding a lamp), 
guard and strap or hook or other device for suspension or carrying. It 
does not include portable lampstands, brackets and similar fittings of a 
decorating nature although they may be movable and not fixed. 

3. RATING 

3.1 Voltage Rating — The preferred voltages shall be 240, 24, 12 and

4. MATERIALS 

4.1 The relevant provisions of 7.11 and 7.12 of IS: 302-1973* shall apply 
in addition to those specified in 4.2 to 4.7 of this standard. 

4.2 Body and Handle — The body and handle of the hand-lamp shall 
be made of suitably treated hardwood or other suitable non-hygroscopic 
insulating material capable of withstanding rough usage and the heat 
generated by the lamp* The materials used shall be such that it shall not 
be uncomfortable to hold the handle. 

4.2.1 There shall be at least 10 cm of clear gripping space on the 
handle. 

4.2.2 The following precautions shall be taken to ensure that it is 
impossible for a screw or other non-current-carrying metal part to act as a 
conductor from the inside to the outside of the body or handle: 

a) Any metal screw or fixing entering the body from the outside shall 
not penetrate within 4 mm of the inner wall of the cavity in the 
centre of the body. 

b) Any metal screw or fixing in contact with an interior metal part 
entering the body from the inside shall not penetrate within 4 mm 
from the outer surface. 

c) If any metal screw or fixing inserted from the inside of the body 
projects to the outside for the purpose, for instance, of securing 
an external metal part, the head shall be recessed into the body 
by at least 4 mm clear and, in addition, shall be permanently 
shrouded in such a manner as to exclude the possibility of electri- 
cal contact with any interior metal part. 

4.3 Guard and Shield 

4.3.1 The guard shall be of suitable design and of adequate strength to 
provide adequate protection for the lamp and lampholder and to provide 
a convenient base on which to stand the hand-lamp. The guard may be 
open at the end. The guard, if of metal, shall be separated from the 
metal parts of the lampholder and from any metal parts in contact there- 
with by proper insulation. 

4.3.2 When required, a shield to obscure the light in the desired direc- 
tions may be fitted inside or outside the guard. The shield, if fitted, shall 
be arranged so as not to make contact with the lampholder or lamp. 

4.3.3 The guard shall be so designed as to provide easy means of lamp 
replacement preferably without necessitating the removal of the guard 
from the body. 

4.4 Switches — Hand-lamps shall not be provided with a switch.

4.5 Bushes or Bellmonth Ends — The hole through which the flexible 
cord or cable enters the body of the hand-lamp shall be of bellmouth type 
or so shaped as to prevent or avoid the abrasion of the conductor or its 
insulation which may lead to its electrical or mechanical failure. Bushes, 
if used, shall be securely fixed in position. 

4.6 Hanging Device — Every hand-lamp shall be provided with a loop 
or hook by means of which it may be hung up when not in use. Any 
such device for independent suspension shall be securely attached and 
shall be so positioned that it is not capable of penetrating the guard and 
causing breakage of the lamp. 

NOTE — In no case shall the lamp be meant to be suspended on its flexible cord. 

4.7 Lampholder — The lampholdcr shall be suitable for the voltage and 
wattage rating of the lamp used in the hand-lamp. If of bayonet type, it 
shall comply with the requirements of IS : 1258-1958*. It shall be easy to 
wire and shall be secured in such a manner that neither longitudinal nor 
circumferential movement is possible when the lamp is inserted or re- 
moved. Such fixing shall be independent of the clamping action of any 
detachable guard.

7. MARKING AND INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE 

7.1 Each hand-lamp shall be marked indelibly and clearly with the 
following particulars: 

a) Name or trade-mark of the manufacturer, 

b) Rated voltage and the maximum wattage of the lamp that could 
be used, 

c) Makers model or type reference, and 

d) Country of manufacture. 

7.1.1 The hand-lamps may also be marked with the ISI Certification 
Mark. 

NOTE — The use of the ISI Certification Mark is governed by the provisions of the 
Indian Standards Institution (Certification Marks) Act and the Rules and Regulations 
made thereunder. The ISI Mark on products covered bv an Indian Standard 
conveys the assurance that they have been produced to comply with the requirements 
of that standard under a well-defined system of inspection, testing and quality control 
which is devised and supervised by ISI and operated by the producer. ISI marked 
products are also continuously checked by ISI for conformity to that standard as a 
further safeguard. Details of conditions under which a licence for the use of the ISI 
Certification Mark may be granted to manufacturers or processors, may be obtained 
from the Indian Standards Institution. 

7.2 Marking of the Earth Terminal — The symbol ' J^ ' shall be 
marked permanently on or adjacent to the earth terminal. 

7.3 Instructions for Use — Each hand-lamp shall be provided with 
necessary instructions including precautions to be taken for its use.

8.3.2 Testfor Mechanical Strength — The hand-lamps shall be allowed to 
fall six times vertically from a height of 30 cm on to a wooden floor or 
solid wooden bench. 

8.3.2.1 There shall be no damage to the handle and guard which 
affects the working of the lamp and no points shall be broken or cracked.

temperature of any material or insulation or operating part shall not exceed 
the limits given in Table 3 of IS : 302-1973*.
