FOREWORD 

This Indian Standard was adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards, after the draft finalized by 
Fasteners for Consumer Goods and Hair Cutting, Shaving, Shearing and AlUed Equipnients 
Sectional Committee had been approved by the Light Mechanical Engineering Division Council. 

The razor blade industry has been feeling the need for a national standard covering the require- 
ments for razor handles due to its increasing demand in the domestic market and popularity 
among the consumers in view of safety and smooth shaving. These shaving systems are also being 
exported in large numbers and the standard would help those exporting and intending to export 
these types of shaving systems.

5 MATERIAL 

5.1 The razor handles may be made of high 
impact heat-resistant plastic or corrosion resistant 
metal or combination of the two. 

6 DESIGN, CONSTRUCTION AND 
WORKMANSHIP 

6.1 Twin blade razor handle shall fit into the 
accommodate twin blade cartridges conforming 
to IS 13031 : 1990. When assembled with the 
cartridge this should be evenly balanced. Car- 
tridge alignment pattern shall be with sliding 
type or swivel type. The handle shall be knurled, 
grooved or serrated to provide a firm grip when 
used in a manner consistent with normal use. 
The length of the handle and the area of cross- 
section shall be of minimum 100 mm and 30 mm^ 
respectively measured on the largest cross-section. 
The razor design shall provide proper positioning 
of cartridges for satisfactory shaving. 

6.2 Twin blade razor handle shall provide a 
secure fit and shall not loosen while shaving. 

6.3 Razor handle shall be durable and easy to 
operate. 

6.4 Cartridge changing in sliding type is by 
sliding the razor slide at the back of the cartridge 
and in swivel type by inserting the razor finger 
at the groove provided at the back of the 
cartridges. 

6.5 All components of the razor handle shall be 
smoothly finished and serrated, grooved or 
knurled. Surfaces shall be free from sharp edges 
or burrs. The slider shall be perpendicular to 
the handle axis. 



7 FINISH 

7.1 The components of safety razors made of 
non-ferrous metal, except aluminium, shall be 
plated chromium over nickel having a thickness 
of nickel plating of minimum 20 microns. For 
other requirements the nickel plating shall 
conform to Service Grade No. 3 of IS 1068 : 1993. 
The aluminium components shall be anodized in 
accordance with Grade AC5 of IS 1868 : 1982. 

8 TEST 

-8.1 Sliding groove and width shall be checked 
with a suitable gauge.

indelibly marked either with the manufacturers

seller.
