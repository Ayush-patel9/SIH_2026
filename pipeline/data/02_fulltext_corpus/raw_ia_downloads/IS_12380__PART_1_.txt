1. SCOPE 

1.1 This standard specifies the test schedule and 
requirements applicable for radio frequency 
connectors of N series. 

2. TERMINOLOGY 

2.1 For the purpose of this standard, the terms 
and definitions given in IS : 1885 ( Part 40 )- 
1974* and IS : 5054-1988t shall apply. 

3. MATERIALS, CONSTRUCTION AND 
WORKMANSHIP 

3.1 The construction and workmanship shall be 
in accordance with 4 of IS : 5054-1988t. The 
materials and finish shall be in accordance with 
the relevant specification.

according to this standard, shall be designated

5.1 The connectors shall be marked in accordance 
with 6 of IS: 5054-1988*.

6.1 The connector shall be suitably packaged to 
withstand normal handling and transit hazards.

of contract.
