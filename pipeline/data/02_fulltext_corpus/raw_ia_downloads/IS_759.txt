0.3 A swell, one of the various parts of a loom, 
is fixed in each of the two shuttle boxes fitted on 
each side of the loom. The swell performs three 
functions: 

a) It acts as a brake on the shuttle at the end 
of its traverse across the loom, 

b) It prevents the shuttle from rebounding out 
of the shuttle box where the shutde halts 
momentarily at the end of its traverse 
across the loom, and 

c) It holds the shuttle at its proper place near 
the picker to receive the stroke of the 
picker for its next traverse in the reverse 
direction.
