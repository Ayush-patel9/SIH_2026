EXPLANATORY NOTE 

As for agricultural purposes diesel monoset are generally used, the Committee felt the need of 
preparing separate standard engine monoset pumps. Diesel engine monoset pumps are centrifugal 
pumps of single suction with driving diesel engine designed and built as complete unit on one shaft 
(that is the crank shaft and the pump shaft are one and same). This eliminates pump shaft, pumps 
bearing and make the combined unit simple and compact. The requirement of monoset centrifugal 
pumps fixed with electric motors are covered in IS : 9079-1979 'Specification for monoset pumps for 
clear, cold, fresh water for agricultural purposes'.
