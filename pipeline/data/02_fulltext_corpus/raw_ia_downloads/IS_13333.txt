FOREWORD 

This Indian Standard was adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards, after the draft finalized by 
the Pesticides Sectional Committee had been approved by the Food and Agriculture Division 
Council. 

Metribuzin WP is largely used as herbicide in agriculture. 

Metribuzin WP is generally manufactured to contain 70 percent ( m/m ) of metribuzin. 

In the preparation of this standard, due consideration has been given to the provisions of the 
Insecticides Act, 1968 and the Rules framed thereunder. However, this standard is subject to 
the restrictions imposed under the Act and Rules, wherever applicable. 

For the purpose of deciding whether a particular requirement of this standard is complied with, 
the final value, observed or calculated, expressing the result of a test or analysis, shall be rounded 
off in accordance with IS 2 : 1960 'Rules for rounding off numerical values ( revised) 9 . The 
number of significant places retained in the rounded off value should be the same as that of the 
specified value in this standard.

6 SAMPLING 

When a bulk quantity of the material is offered 
for inspection, representative samples of the 
material shall be drawn as prescribed in IS 
10627 : 1983 and tested within 90 days of its 
manufacturing. The criteria for conformity 
shall be as given in IS 10627 : 1983. When the 
material is offered for inspection after 90 days 
of its manufacturing, sampling shall be done 
according to IS 10627 : 1983. However, the 
criteria for conformity of the material when 
tested, shall be the limits of tolerances, as 
applicable over the declared nominal value, 
given under 3.3.1 of this standard.

7.1 Tests shall be carried out by the methods 
referred to in col 4 and 5 of Table 1, 7.2 
and 7.3. 

7.2 For determination of metribuzin content, 
weigh accurately a quantity of sample so as to 
contain 0-50 — 0-55 g of matribuzin into a 
100-ml beaker. Stir with 20 ml portions of 
acetone four time and filter through a G 4 
crucible under vacuum containing a bed of 
hyfio supercel or equivalent. Collect the 
filtrate in a 100-ml volumetric flask. 



7.3 Suspensibility Test 

7.3.1 For carrying out suspensibility test, start 
with 0*75 percent concentration and proceed as 
described in 11.2 of IS 6940 : 1982. 

7.3.2 Transfer the bottom 25 ml of the suspen- 
sion and sediment quantitatively to a 250-ml 
separatory funnel using 25 ml ether. Extract 
the metribuzin with 2 portions each of 50 ml 
ether. Filter the ether extract through a bed 
of anhydrous sodium sulphate using a funnel 
directly into a 250-ml Erlenmeyer flask. 
Evaporate the ether on a water-bath and 
dissolve the residue in the flask in 5 ml acetone. 
Add to it 5 ml internal standard solution 
( see 7.3.3 ) and shake well to homogenize. 

7.3.3 Take 10 ml of the internal standard solu- 
tion (see IS 13332 : 1992) into a 50-ml volumetric 
flask and dilute up to the mark with acetone. 

7.3.4 Accurately weigh 0*25 g metribuzin 
reference standard into a 25-ml volumetric 
flask,. dilute up to the mark with acetone and 
shake well to homogenize. Take out 5 ml of 
the solution with a pipette into a 10-ml volume- 
tric flask, add 5 ml of the internal standard 
solution ( see 7.3.3 ) and mix well.
