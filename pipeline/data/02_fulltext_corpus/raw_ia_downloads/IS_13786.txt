DETERMINATION OF BENOMYL CONTENT 



A-l PRINCIPLE 

Benomyl is extracted by dissolving in chloroform 
and filtration employing a millipore filter. 

The linear absorbance of the infra-red light 
source is measured at the 1140 cm" 1 wavenumber 
(considered to be a stretching frequency involv- 
ing the ester grouping ) and the concentration 
of benomyl is calculated relative to a predetermi- 
ned absorbance value of a reference standard. 

A-2 APPARATUS 

A-2.1 Infra-red Spectrophotometer 

A-2.2 Absorption Cells 

Scdium chloride cells having an internal light 
path of about 0*20 mm. 

A-2.3 Hypodermic Syringe 

1 ml capacity. 

A-2.4 Mechanical Shaker 

A-2*5 Millipore Filter 

A-2.6 Filter Discs 

Millipore 13 mm, pore size 0.45 pt. 

A-2.7 Specimen Bottles 

25x95 mm size and with screw caps. 
A-3 REAGENTS 

A-3.1 Basic Alumina 

A-3.2 Chloroform 

A-3.3 Reference Standard Benomyl 

Of known purity. 

A-4 PROCEDURE 

A-4.1 Preparation of Reference Standard Solution 

Weigh about 0.22 g reference standard ■ benomyl 
in a specimen bottle and accurately add 25 ml 
chloroform and immediately close tightly with 
a screw cap. Clamp the vial to a mechanical 
shaker and shake for 5 minutes. Allow the 
solution to stand for 4 more minutes. With the 
aid of a 1 ml syringe, filter the solution through 
the millipore filter into the absorption cell. 



A-4.2 Preparation of Sample Solution 

Weigh accurately a quantity of sample so as to 
contain 022 g benomyl and prepare the solution 
following the same procedure as has been 
described in A-4.1. 

NOTE — The solution may be centrifuged for 2 
minutes before filtering for obtaining accurate 
results. It will be essential to do this exercise in 
cases where benomyl content is less than 50 percent 
by mass. 

A-4.3 Calibration 

A-4.3.1 Fill an absorption cell with chloroform. 
Adjust the spectrophotometer for the optimum 
instrument setting with respect to gain, slit 
width, response, speed and drum drive. Make 
scan with chloroform in the cell over the 
wavelength region 1180 cm -1 to 1120 cm -1 . 
A-4.3.2 Turn the wavelength scale back to its 
original setting. Fill the cell with reference 
standard solution ( A-4.1 ). Make a scan in the 
wavelength region 1180 cm" 1 to 1120 cm" 1 
with the same setting as previously used 
( A-4.3.1 ). Determine the linear absorbance of 
the solution at 1 140 cm" 1 using chloroform blank 
absorbance as the baseline. 

A-4.3.3 Turn the wavelength scale back to its 
original setting. Fill the cell with sample 
solution ( A-4.2 ). Make a scan in the wave- 
length region 1180 cm" 1 to 1120 cm" 1 , with the 
same setting as previously used ( A-4.3.1 ). 
Determine the linear absorbance of the solution 
at H 40 cm" 1 using chloroform blank absorbance 
as the baseline.
