This  Standard  was  first  issued  in  1969.  It  was  revised  in  1975  with  a  view  to  bringing  the 
standard  up  to  dale  and  to  align  it  with  the  specifications  for  basic  synthetic  food  colour  issued  by

the  tolerance  on  the  positive  side  was  retained  at  the  earlier  level. 

A  limited  number  of  synthetic  food  colours  have  been  permitted  under  the  Pretention  of  Food  Adul-

Government  of  India  under  Prevention  of  Food  Adulteration  Act,  1954.  These  rules,  inter-alia 
prescribe:
