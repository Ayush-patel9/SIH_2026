TENDER NOTICE NIT-GOV-2026-041
1. SCOPE: Supply, testing, installation, and commissioning of equipment conforming to national standards for Fire & Safety.
2. MANDATORY SPECIFICATIONS:
   a) Primary material supply must conform to IS 15683 (latest edition).
   b) Allied items and sub-assemblies shall conform to IS 2171 and IS 2878.
   c) Workmanship, installation guidelines, and safety criteria shall strictly follow IS 3844 and IS 13039.
3. QUALITY CONTROL & INSPECTION: All bidders must submit valid BIS licenses / CRS registration certificates and NABL accredited lab test reports.
