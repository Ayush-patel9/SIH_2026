TENDER NOTICE NIT-GOV-2026-045
1. SCOPE: Supply, testing, installation, and commissioning of equipment conforming to national standards for Automotive & Transport.
2. MANDATORY SPECIFICATIONS:
   a) Primary material supply must conform to IS 2553 (latest edition).

3. QUALITY CONTROL & INSPECTION: All bidders must submit valid BIS licenses / CRS registration certificates and NABL accredited lab test reports.
