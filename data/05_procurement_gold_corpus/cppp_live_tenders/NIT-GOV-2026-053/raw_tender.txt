TENDER NOTICE NIT-GOV-2026-053
1. SCOPE: Supply, testing, installation, and commissioning of equipment conforming to national standards for Paints & Coatings.
2. MANDATORY SPECIFICATIONS:
   a) Primary material supply must conform to IS 2932 (latest edition).
   b) Allied items and sub-assemblies shall conform to IS 101 and IS 5410.
   c) Workmanship, installation guidelines, and safety criteria shall strictly follow IS 15489 and IS 10151.
3. QUALITY CONTROL & INSPECTION: All bidders must submit valid BIS licenses / CRS registration certificates and NABL accredited lab test reports.
