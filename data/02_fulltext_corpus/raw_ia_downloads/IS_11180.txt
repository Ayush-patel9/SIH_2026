PART 1 SQUARE AND HEXAGONAL KELLYS 



1. Scope — Lays down the requirements for square and hexagonal kellys manufactured as a single 
piece used for transmitting the rotational movement from the rotary table to the drill stem. 

2. Terminology 

2.1 ^/ppe^(ypse^— Cylindrical extremity of the kelly. having left-hand box connection for coupling 
with the swivel. 

2.2 Lower Upset — Cylindrical extremity of the kelly. having right-hand pin connection for coupling 
with the drill-pipe string, 

2.3 Length of Drive — Part of the kelly limited by the upper and lower upsets taking over the rota- 
tional movement from the rotary table. 

3. Types — Kellys are manufactured with either square or hexagonal drive section: 

a) Square kelly is designated as type *S', and 

b) Hexagonal kelly is designated as type *H\ 

4. Dimensions 

4.1 Square Kellys — The dimensions of square kelly shall be as specified in Table 1. 

4.2 Hexagonal Kellys — The dimensions of hexagonal kelly shall be as specified in Table 2. 

5. Designation — A kelly with square drive section of size 88'9 shall be designated as: 

Kelly 88-9 SIS: 11180 

6. IHaterial — Kellys shall be manufactured from alloy steel conforming to 37Mn 2 or 40 Cr 1 of 
IS: 1570-1961 'Schedules for wrought steels for general engineering purposes' or equivalent 
having following properties after heat treatment. 

Yield stress, MIn = 686 N/mm» 

Tensile strength, Min = 882 N/mm« 

Minimum elongation on test specimen with L^ = 5'65 V5o = 11 percent 

Longitudinal impact test ( mean on three specimen ) KU 300/2 = 50 J 
Transverse impact test KCU ( mean on three specimen ) KU 300/2 =^ 30 J 
Hardness, HB, MIn = 270 

7. General Requirements 

7-1 The outside surface of the kellys shall be sound, free from cracks, slivers, laminations, inclu- 
sions or other visible defects liable to affect the quality and the strength of the kellys. 

7.2 Heat Treatment - Full length of kellys shall be treated. If agreed between the purchaser and 
the manufacturer, the heat treatment may be limited to two ends for lengths of approximately 
1 500 mm. 

7.3 Straightness 

7.3.1 Local deflections — The local length deflection of kelly shall not exceed 1 mm/2 000 mm 
length of kellys.

7-3.2 Total length deflection —The total length deflection of kellys shall not exceed 0*000 5 mm 
per millimetre of length. Final reading of total deflection shall be rounded off to two decimal places 
in accordance with IS : 2-1960 * Rules for rounding off numerical values ( revised ) *. 

7.4 Til reads 

7-4.1 Kelly threads shall be free from tears, cuts and shoulders or any other defects which break 
the continuity of the threads. 

7.4.2 The threads shall be electroplated or processed by some other acceptable method to mini- 
mize galling and corrosion damage. 

7-4-3 Threading, gauging and inspection of threads shall be carried out according to ' Specifica- 
tion for rotary shouldered connections ( under preparation )\ 

7-5 If required by purchaser, kellys may be provided with box connection at the lower upset. 

7.6 If agreed between the purchaser and the manufacturer kellys may be provided with weld-on 
type joints.

8.2 Mechanical Tests 

8.2.1 Sampling — An additional length of 120 mm shall ba left at the lower end of the kelly: This 
additional portion shall be cut off after heat treatment of kelly in order to prepare test specimens 
as follows: 

a) Specimen for tenslfe tests hall have their axis approximately in the middle of the test 
portion, and 

b) Specimen for impact test shall be taken tangential to the bore. 

8.2.2 Tensile test shall be carried out in accordance with IS : 1608-1972 'Method for tensile test- 
ing of steel products ( first revision )'. 

8.2.3 Impact test 

8.2.3.1 Three longitudinal impact test shall be carried out in accordance with IS: 1499-1977 
'Method for Charpy impact test ( U-notch ) for metals ( first revision )' on specimen taken from one 
sample from a lot of 10 kellys. A different number of longitudinal impact test can be conducted 
if agreed upon between the purchaser and the manufacturer. 

8.2.3.2 Transverse impact test shall be carried out in accordance with IS : 1499-1977 in case of 
hot rolled kellys or if required specifically by the purchaser. 

8.2.4 Hardness test — Hardness test shall be carried out in accordance with IS ; 1500-1983 
' Method for brineil hardness test for metallic materials ( second revision )' . Five readings shall be 
made, one at each end and three more equally spaced along the length of kelly. in case heat 
treatment is limited to two ends ( see 7.2 ) two hardness readings shall be made one at each end. 

8.2.5 Reiests — If a sample is rejected on inspection, all tests shall be repeated on a sample 
taken from a second kelly in the same lot. 

8.2.5.1 If second sample is also rejected, each kelly shall be tested separately, 

9. Marking — Each kelly shall be die stamped as follows: 

a) On the upper upset with the mark of the manufacturer and the size and style of 
connection, and 

b) On the lower upset with the size and style of the connection. 

9.1 ISI Certification Marking — Details available with the Indian Standards Institution. 

10. Packing 

10.1 The threads of the kellys shall be covered with anticorrosive lubricant to protect the thread 
from damage under climatic conditions specified by the purchaser. 

10.2 In addition, the kellys shall be provided with thread protectors. The design and material of 
the thread protectors shall be as agreed to between the purchaser and the manufacturer. 

10-3 If required by purchaser the kelly may be supplied in metallic tubes provided inside with rigid 
supports.
