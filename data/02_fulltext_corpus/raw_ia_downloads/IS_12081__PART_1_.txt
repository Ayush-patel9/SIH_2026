0.    FOREWORD 

0.1  This  Indian  Standard  (  Part  1  )lwas  adopted  by  the  Bureau  of  Indian 
Standards  on  3  August  1987,  after  the  draft  finalized  by  the  Cement  and 
Concrete  Sectional  Committee  had  been  approved  by  the  Civil 
Engineering  Division  Council. 

0.2  In  recent  years  there  has  been  a  growing  awareness  that  exposure 
to  asbestos  dust  can  have  harmful  effects  an  the  health  of  workers. 
Accordingly,  a  number  of  standards  giving  technical  guidance  on  how 
the  risk  of  exposure  to  asbestos  dust  can  be  prevented,  controlled  or 
minimized  have  been  taken  up  for  formulation.  It  has  also  been  felt 
necessary  that  some  pictorial  warning  signs  and  precautionary  notices 
shall  be  used  at  workplaces  involving  asbestos  and  asbestos  products, 
packages  containing  asbestos  and  asbestos  products,  and  also  on  the 
asbestos  products,  in  order  to  caution  the  workers  and  users  about  the 
potential  health  hazards  of  asbestos.  Keeping  this  in  view,  this  stau'^ard 
regarding  pictorial  warning  signs  and  precautionary  notices  has  bt^en 
formulated  in  two  parts  as  follows: 

a)  Recommendations  for  pictorial  warning  signs  and  precautionary 
notices  for  asbestos  and  products  containing  asbestos:  Part  1 
Workplaces;  and 

b)  Recommendations  for  pictorial  warning  signs  and  precautionary 
notices  for  asbestos  and  products  containing  asbestos:  Part  2 
Asbestos  and  its  products. 

0.3  In  the  formulation  of  this  standard  due  weightage  has  been  given  to 
international  co-ordination  among  the  standards  and  practices  prevail- 
ing in  different  countries  in  addition  to  relating  it  to  the  practices  in  the 
field  in  this  country.

1.  SCOPE 

1.1  This  standard  (  Part  I  )  deals  with  pictorial  warning  signs  and 
precautionary  notices  to  be  used  at  workplaces  involving  asbestos  and 
products  containing  asbestos. 

2.  OBJECT 

2.1  The  object  of  this  standard  is  to  caution  those  engaged  in  handling 
of  asbestos  and  products  containing  asbestos  so  as  to  enable  them  to  take 
necessary  precautionary  measures  against  the  risk  from  exposure  to 
asbestos  dust. 

3.  LABELLING 

3.1  Workplaces  with  a  potential  to  release  airborne  asbestos  dust  shall 
be  clearly  indicated  as  asbestos  dust  exposure  area  through  the  use  of 
well-displayed  pictorial  warning  signs  and  notices.  The  purpose  of  these 
warning  signs/notices  is  to  ensure  that  asbestos  is  not  worked  upon  with- 
out appropriate  precautions  and  to  restrict  the  entry  to  authorized 
persons  only. 

3.2  Where  airborne  asbestos  dust  concentration  is  above  the  action  level 
but  below  the  permissible  exposure  limit  (  see  IS  :  11451-1986*  ),  pictorial 
warning  sign  and  notice  given  in  Fig.  1  shall  be  used. 

3.3  Where  airborne  asbestos  dust  concentration  exceeds  the  permissible 
exposure  limit,  pictorial  warning  sign  and  notice  given  in  Fig,  2  shall 
be  used. 

3.4  Size  —  The  dimensions  of  the  pictorial  warning  sign  shall  be  not 
less  than  300  x  4^0  mm  for  Fig.  I  and  300  X  600  mm  for  Fig.  2.
