PART 2 TELEX DATA TRANSMISSION 



1 Scope 

This part of ISO 9711 specifies a uniform data infor- 
mation system for transmitting all necessary data 
and loading plans in a short period of time to the 
next loading/discharging point using a telex or tele- 
copy (telefax) communication system. 

It is applicable to all standard freight containers 
covered by International Standards and should, 
wherever appropriate and practicable, be applied to 
those containers not covered by International Stan- 
dards. 

It applies only to vessels stowing containers 
longitudinally.

of the respective ship, including draught, trim sta-

arrival, etc.

and

3.2 Container-related data 

The second section of the report shall provide infor- 
mation about the container and the cargo. 

The container location shall be reported as follows: 

— bay numbers, \n accordance with 
ISO 9711-1:1990, 3.1, shall be given at the bottom 
of the structured report; 

— row numbers, in accordance with 
ISO 9711-1:1990, 3.2, shall be given at the top of 
the structured report and shall follow the data in 
accordance with 3.1; 

— layer or tier numbers, in accordance with 
ISO 9711-1:1990, 3.3, shall be given on the left- 
hand side of the structured report. 

If all da^a for one bay cannot be placed within the 
telex format width^), the bay plan should be divided 
into 

— one plan for containers on deck, and 

— one plan for containers under deck. 

These plans shall be subdivided into two pages: one 
for containers above, respectively under, the port- 
side and centre hatch covers and the other for those 
above, respectively under, the starboard-side hatch 
covers. 

3.3 Cargo-related data 

The report is structured in such a way that it re- 
sennbles the actual layout of the vessel, in accord- 
ance with the stowage plan specified in 
ISO 9711-1:1990, clause 3. The report is subdivided 
into fields, each of which represents one container. 
Each field shall accommodate seven digits per line 
and five lines (see figure 1 and figure 2). A field size 
of 21 mm in width and 26 mm in height is recom- 
mended for most telex systems.

The fifth line shall be reserved for cargo-related Information. 
Arternatlvely, the foMowing Information shall t>e provided: 

— Identification of <iangerous goods; 

— Identification of temperature range of reefer cargo; 

— Identification of out-of-gauge cargo. 

a) Dangerous goods 

The first left-hand digit shall be the letter "D" to Indicate the 
Identification of dangerous goods. The following two digits 
shall t>e used to identify the hazard class in accordance with 
the IMDG code of the I MO. For containers loaded with differ- 
ent types of dangerous goods, the remaining four digits may 
t>e used to indicate two further hazard classes. 

b) Temperature range of reefer cargo 

For sensitive cargoes requiring more precise temperature 
limits, the temperature scale code (for example, C for degrees 
Celsius) shall be followed by three digits representing the 
lower limit temperature only, In tenths of a degree. For ex- 
ample C+135 would represent a sensitive cargo with a lower 
temperature limit of + 13,5 "C. For such sensitive cargoes, It 
Is clear that any upper Hmtt should be as do^e as practically 
possible to the lower limit. 

c) Out-of-gauge cargo 

1) In the case of over-width cargo, the fourth digit shall be 
the letter "W" to indicate over-width. The left-hand three 
digits shall be used to show over-width on the left-hand 
side of the container (left-hand viewed from door end to 
closed end wall of container). The right-hand three digits 
shall be used to indicate over-width on the right-hand side 
of the container. Dimensions shal'l be expressed in centi- 
metres. 

2) In the case of over-height, the first digit of the fifth line 
shall be the letter "H" . The following three digits shall 
be used to show over-height In centimetres. 

3) In the case of both over-width and over-height, this shall 
be indicated as follows: the first three digits for over- 
height; the remaining four digits for total over-width, for 
example H25VV080. 

d) For special cargo Information which cannot be condensed to 
7 digits or which should l>e transmitted without abbreviation, 
the letter "X" should be shown in the fifth line and repeated 
at the end of the report with full text.
