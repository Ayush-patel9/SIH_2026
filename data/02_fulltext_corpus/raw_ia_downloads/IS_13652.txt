FOREWORD 

This Indian Standard was adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards, after the draft finalized 
by the Bulk Handling Equipment Sectional Committee had been approved by the Heavy 
Mechanical Engineering Division Council. 

This Indian Standard has laid down the data that is required to be supplied by the supplier/ 
manufacturer of the equipment to the purchaser. This standard can be considered as an adjunct 
to IS 12140 : 1987 'Data sheet for selection of dumpers'. This standard is aimed to provide a 
uniform practice for giving information by supplier/manufacturer to the purchaser either at the 
time of enquiry or order.
