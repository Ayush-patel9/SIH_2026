PART 1 GENERAL REQUIREMENTS AND TESTS 
0. FOREWORD 

0.1 This Indian Standard ( Part 1 ) was adopted by the Indian Standards 
Institution on 22 February 1983, after the draft finalized by the Trans- 
formers and Inductors for Electronics Equipment Sectional Committee 
had been approved by the Electronics and Telecommunication Division 
Council. 

0.2 The object of this standard ( Part 1 ) is to establish uniform require- 
ments for the electrical, naechanical and climatic properties and test 
methods for frame output transformers used with television picture tubes. 

0.3 Detailed specification for the frame output transformers shall cover the 
specific requirements and shall form other parts of this standard. 

0.4 For the purpose of deciding whether a particular requirement of this 
standard is complied with, the final value, observed or calculated, expres- 
sing the result of a test, shall be rounded off in accordance with IS : 2- 
1960*. The number of significant places retained in the rounded off 
value should be the same as that of the specified value in this standard. 



1. SCOPE 

1.1 This standard ( Part 1 ) relates to general requirements and methods 
of test for judging the mechanical, electrical and environmental properties 
of frame output transformers primarily intended for use with television 
picture tubes. 

2. TERMINOLOGY 

2.0 For the purpose of this standard, the following definitions shall apply.

2.1 Routine Tests — Tests carried out on each frame output trans- 
former to check the requirements which are Hkely to vary during 
production. 

2.2 Acceptance Tests — Tests carried out on frame output transformers 
selected from a lot which has passed the routine tests for the purpose of 
acceptance of the lot. 

2.2.1 Lot — All frame output transformers of the same type, category 
and rating manufactured by the same factory during the same period using 
the same process and materials. 

2.3 Type Tests — Tests carried out to prove conformity with the reqixire- 
ments of this standard. These are intended to prove the general qualities 
and design of a particular type of frame output transformer. 

2.4 Type — A type comprises of products having similar functions manu- 
factured by the same techniques and falling within the manufacturers usual 
range of ratings of these products. 

Note I — Mounting accessories are ignored, providing they have no significant 
effect on the test results. 

Note 2 — Rating cover the combination of: 

a) electrical ratings; 

b) sizes; and 

c) environmental group. 

2.5 Frame Output Transformer — A transformer with silicon steel 
magnetic circuit with primary, secondary and tertiary winding used as 
coupling element between the saw tooth current generator and the 
deflection coil. Tertiary winding can be used for voltage feed back.

5. MATERIALS, CONSTRUCTION AND WORKMANSHIP 

5.1 Materials — The frame output transformers shall be constructed 
from the materials free from flaws and other defects. As far as practicable, 
materials used for construction shall be flame retardant, non-explosive 
non-corrosive. 

5.2 Terminations — The frame output transformers shall be provided 
with leads or any other acceptable terminations. 

5.3 Workmanship ■ — All parts of the frame output transformer shall be 
manufactured in a thoroughly workman like manner and in accordance 
with good engineering practice. 

6. MARKING 

6.1 Each frame output transformer shall be marked with the following 
minimum information: 

a) Type designation and manufacturers name or trade-mark; 

b) Manufacturers code or batch number; and 

c) Terminal identification. 

6.2 In all cases, the carton shall carry the following minimum information: 

a) Type designation and manufacturers name or trade-mark; 

b) Manufacturers code or batch number; and 

c) Name of the component, namely, frame output transformer.

7, TESTS 

7,1 General Conditions for Tests 

7.1.1 General — Tests shall be carried out on the frame output trans- 
former as received from the manufacturer or the supplier. 

7.1.2 Atmospheric Conditions for Testing — ■ Unless otherwise specified, all 
tests shall be carried out under standard atmospheric conditions specified 
in IS : 9C00 ( Part 1 )-1977*. 

7.1.3 Preconditioning — Before measurements are made the frame output 
transformer shall be stored at the measuring temperature and relative 
humidity for a time sufficient to allow the entire frame output transformer 
to reach these conditions. The recovery period called for after condition- 
ing is adequate for this purpose. 

7.1.4 Corrections to be Applied — When measurements are made at an 
ambient temperature other than the reference temperature the results 
shall, where necessary, be corrected to the reference temperature. The 
ambient temperature during the test shall be stated in the test report. 

7.1.5 Other Precautions — ■ During measurements the frame output trans- 
former shall not be exposed to draughts, direct sun-rays or other influences 
likely to cause errors. 

7.1.6 Test Frequency — When a test frequency is specified with no 
tolerance, the frequency used shall be within 1 percent of the specified 
value.

7.3 General Tests 

7.3.1 Visual Examination — The frame output transformer shall be 
visually examined for workmanship, finish, condition and marking and 
these shall be satisfactory. 

7.3.2 Dimensions — The dimensions shall be checked for compliance 
with those specified in the relevant specification and as per manufacturers 
drawings where the requirements are not covered. 

7.4 Electrical Tests 

7.4.1 Continuity of Windings — The windings of the frame output trans- 
former shall be tested for electrical continuity by an suitable method. The 
winding shall be continuous. 

7.4.2 DC Resistance — The dc resistance shall be measured with a 
resistance bridge or other suitable test equipment. The test current through 
the specimen shall be as small as practical considering the sensitivity of the 
indicating instruments, unless the current or voltage is specified. When it 
is important that the temperature of the specimen shall not rise appreciably 
during the measurements, the test voltage shall be applied uninterruptedly 
for as short a time as practicable, but in no case for more than 5 seconds, 
unless otherwise specified. The measurement shall be made at or 
corrected to 25°C. The value shall be within the tolerance limits as 
specified.

7.6 Solderability — The frame output transformer provided with tag and 
lead type terminations shall - be subjected to the soldering iron test 
( Method 3 ) with a soldering iron of suitable size in accordance with Sec 1 
of IS ; 9000 ( Part XVIII )-1981*. 

At the end of the test, the component shall be visually examined. It 
shall not show any loosening of connections, melting or seepage of sealing 
material or any other deterioration. The continuity of the winding shall 
be checked. 

7.7 Robustness of Terminations 

7.7.1 Tensile Test on all Terminations — All terminations shall be sub- 
jected to the test in accordance with Sec 1 of IS : 9000 ( Part XIX)-1978*. 
The loading weight, the duration of the test and the number of bands 
shall be as specified in the relevant specification. In the case of pliable 
terminations, the bending test shall be carried out in accordance with 
Sec 3 of IS : 9000 ( Part XIX )-1978*. 

7.8 Bump — The frame output transformer shall be mounted as specified 
in Sec 2 of IS : 9000 ( Part VII )-1979*. Taking care that the terminations 
are not stressed and that equal number of samples are bumped in each of 
the three principal axes. Samples shall be subjected to 1 000 bumps at an 
acceleration of 250 m/s^, unless otherwise specified. After the test, the 
frame output transformer shall be visually examined and shall not show 
any damage or deterioration. The continuity of the winding shall be 
checked. 

7.9 Vibration — The frame output transformer shall be mounted and 
tested as required in IS : 9000 ( Part VIII )-1981* taking care that the 
terminations are not stressed and that equal number of samples are 
subjected to vibration test in each of the three principal axes. The 
vibration severity should be 10 to 55 Hz with duration of six hours. 

After the test, the frame output transformer shall be visually 
examined and there shall not be any damage or deterioration. The 
continuity of the winding shall be checked. The dc resistance and 
inductance shall be measured and should be within specified limits. 
Voltage proof test shall also be carried out. There shall be no change in 
insulation resistance when measured at the end of this test.

7.10 Climatic Tests 

7.10.1 Climatic Sequence 

7.10.2 Dry Heat — The frame output transformer shall be subjected to 
the dry heat test in accordance with Sec 5 of IS : 9000 ( Part III )-1977*. 
The temperature of the test chamber shall be maintained at the maximum 
category temperature of the frame output transformer. The frame output 
transformer shall be on full load during exposure if specified in the relevant 
specification. While still at the high temperature and during the last half 
hour of exposure, the insulation resistance shall be measured and shall be 
not less than the value specified in the relevant specification. 

After recovery the frame output transformer shall be visually 
examined for any damage or deterioration. The insulation resistance shall 
be measured and it shall not deviate from the values obtained therein. 

7.10.3 Damp Heat ( Cyclic ) ( First Cycles ) — The frame output trans- 
former shall be subjected to the first cycle of this test in accordance with 
Sec 2 of IS : 9000 ( Part V )-1981*. The duration of the recovery period 
shall be one and a half hours. After recovery, the frame output trans- 
former shall be visually examined for any damage or deterioration, 

7.10.4 Cold Test — The fram_e output transform^er shall be subjected to 
the cold test in accordance with Sec 4 of IS : 9000 ( Part II )-197"7.* The 
temperature of the chamber shall be maintained at the minimum category 
temperature. The duration of recovery period shall be one and a half 
hours. After recovery the frame output transformer shall be visually 
examined and there shall not be any damage or deterioration. The 
following tests shall be carried out: 

a) Gonunuity of the winding; and 

b) Voltage proof. 

7.10.5 Air Pressure ( Low ) — The frame output transformer shall be 
subjected to low air pressure test in accordance with IS : 9000 ( Part XIII )- 
1981*. The pressure shall be appropriate to the category and the duration 
shall be 5 minutes. 

During the period of exposure, a test voltage equal to 120 percent of 
the working voltage shall be applied between terminals of the windings.

7.11 Damp Heat { Steady State ) — The frame output transformer shall 
be subjected to the damp heat ( steady state ) test in accordance with 
IS : 9000 ( Part IV )-1979*. The duration of the exposure shall be in

During the test, loading if required, shall be specified in the relevant 
specification. After recovery the components shall be subjected to visual 
examination and the continuity of windings is tested. There shall not be 
any apparent damage. The marking shall be legible and the windings 
shall be continuous. 

The component shall then be subjected to dc resistance, inductance, 
voltage proof and insulation resistance tests. The values shall not be less 
than those specified in the relevant specifications. 

7.12 Mould Growth — The frame output transformer shall be subjected 
to mould growth test in accordance with IS : 9000 ( Part X )-1979*. At 
the end of the test the frame output transformer shall be subjected to visual 
examination and the continuity of windings. There shall not be any 
apparent damage, the markings shall be legible and the winding shall be 
continuous, 

7.13 Salt Mist — The frame output transformers shall be subjected to 
salt mist test in accordance with 7.10 of IS ; 589-1961t. The duration of 
exposure shall be 4 days. After cleaning and recovery, the component 
shall be subjected to a visual examination and the continuity of windings 
shall be tested. There shall not be any apparent damage, the markings 
shall be legible and the windings shall be continuous.
