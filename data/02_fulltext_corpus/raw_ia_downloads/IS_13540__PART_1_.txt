7 TESTS 

7.1 Chemical Analysis 

Suitable quantity of lead pellets shall be drawn 
from each lot to ascertain the chemical composi- 
tion as described in 4. 

7.2 Hardness Test 

Five pellets from each lot shall be drawn as 
random sample to ascertain the hardness as given 
in 5. The lot shall be accepted if all the five 
pellets meet the requirement for hardness. 

7.3 Proof 

Samples from each lot shall be subjected to the 
following proofs. 

7.3.1 Velocity 

Ten random sample pellets shall be fired from a 
fixed Weapon of a good condition and ot a stand- 
ard manufacture. The average velocity shall be 
checked at 10 m and the individual value shall 
not be less than 140 m/sec. The range ( difference 
between the highest and lowest velocity ) bhall 
not be more than 25 m/scc for the same weapon. 

NOTE — A weapon of good condition means that it 
should not have been used for firing more than 5 OOO 
pellets. 

7.3.2 Consistency 

Ten samples each of randomly selected pellets 
shall be fired on four different targets placed 
at 10 m from a fixed weapon oi a good condition 
and of standard manufacture. The diameter of 
the pattern formed shall not exceed 25 mm in 
any case. 

NOTE — A weapon of good condition means that it 
should not have been used for firing more than 5 000 
pellets.
