FOREWORD 

This Indian Standard was adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards, after the draft finalized by 
the Bulk Handling Systems and Equipment Sectional Committee had been approved by the Heavy 
Mechanical Engineering Division Council. 

In industries such as steel, thermal power, cement, etc, uniform quality of the raw material plays 
an important role in the quality of the product and also for the optimum operation of the plant. 
The raw material is therefore first stacked, blended and then reclaimed for feeding to the plant 
for further processing. Reclaiming is done by reclaimers. Bucket wheel reclaimer is a type of 
reclaimer used for this purpose. 

Bucket wheel reclaimer consists of a system of belt conveyors and extendable boom on which a heel 
is fitted. The wheel has a number of buckets around it which dig into the pile of the material and loads 
it onto the conveyor system for feeding to the plant. 

For the purpose of deciding whether a particular requirement of this standard is complied with, the 
final value, observed or calculated, expressing the result of a test or analysis, shall be rounded off in 
accordance with IS 2 : 1960 'Rules for rounding off numerical values { revised )'. The number of 
significant places retained in the rounded off value should be the same as that of the specified 
value in this standard.

push button stations shall be provided near all 
drives for repair and maintenance.

6 BUCKET WHEEL 

6.1 The bucket shape shall be designed as cell or 
cell less type depending 'upon material charac- 
teristics and for proper cutting clearance, taking 
wheel chamber into account for large bucket area 
for better emptying. The wheel shall be inclined 
towards the conveyors to increase wheel chute 
inclination and to achieve better emptying. The 
movement of the bucket shall be uniform and so 
timed that the reclaiming process, filling and dis- 
charge of the bucket operate with maximum 
efficiency. The flow of material shall be almost 
uniform and the end of discharge phase of the 
bucket shall coincide with the beginning of dis- 
charge phase of f&llowing bucket. 

6.2 Buckets shall be made of wear resistant steel or 
steel with wear resistant Uners and fitted with 
replaceable, high abrasive resistant silicon man- 
ganese bucket teeth or tips on wear resistant bucket 
blade. Bucket wheel shall be driven by hydraulic 
motor or electric motor with hydraulic coupling 
through a reduction unit. The bucket wheel drive 
shall be independent of boom conveyor drive and 
equipped with a speed monitor as a safety facility 
on bucket wheel main gearing. 

6.3 If required, bucket wheel assembly may have 
the provision for attaching harrow for blending of 
material. 



6.4 Bucket wheel shall be driven through a torque 
limiting device to prevent overloading of the drive 
by switching off the drive in case of Jamming of the 
buckets in the stockpile. Brakes shall be provided 
on the bucket wheel drive so that wheel docs not 
rotate when the power is off.

7.1 Wire Ropes and Rope Drums 

Wire ropes shall conform to IS 2266 : 1989 and shall 
be of best plough steel. Factor of safely shall be as 
per IS 3177 : 1977. Rope drums and sheaves shall 
conform to the requirements laid down in IS 3177 : 
1977. 

7.2 Bearings 

Allbearings shall beproicctcd against dust and gril. 
Allowance shall be made for impact and side thrust. 
Wherever necessary, spherical scatings and 
separate thrust bearings of suitable dimensions 
shall be used. Antifriction bearing used shall have 
minimum life of 5O000 working hours. 

7.3 Couplings 

Flexible couplings shall be of steel or cast iron of 
suitable grade to withstand transmission of maxi- 
mum torque developed and shall be sized/seiecled 
accordingly. For boom conveyor, high speed 
coupling shall be fluid coupling in case of motor 
rating of 30 kW and above. 

7.4 Shalt 

7.4.1 Shafts and axles shall have sufficient strength, 
rigidity and adequate bearing surfaces. Shafts sub- 
jected to cyclic loading shall be designed for fatigue 
life also. 

7.4.2 All shafts shall be made of carbon or alloy 
steel. Keyways and splines shall conform to relevant 
Indian Standards. 

7.5 Gears and Gearboxes 

Gears in power operated motion shall be 
ground and hardened and shall conform to relevant 
Indian Standards. Gearboxes shall have built in 
lubrication system of either splash or forced type.

(conical) breads, with flanges or any other means as 
and where necessary, to guide the reclaimer effec- 
tively and to prevent derailment. The wheels shall 
be mounted in such a manner as to facilitate 
removal and replacement. 

7.6.2 The track wheels shall be of steel (cast or 
forged) or have steel lyres shrunk on and registered.

SECTION 2 ELECTRO-MECHANICAL 
REQUIREMENTS 

15 GENERAL 

15.1 The bucket wheel reclaimer shall be complete 
with all electrical accessories required for its safe, 
smooth and efficient operation. 

15.2 Only non-hygroscopic materials shall be used 
for insulation. 

15.3 Electrical sys.lem offered shall be suitable for 
the power supply system fault level at that point. 

15.4 Electrical installation work shall be carried 
out through licensed electrical contractors, holding 
valid contractor's licences issued by the concerned 
Statutory Authority and also conforming to 
relevant standards of individual electricals located 
in the machine. 

15.5 All electricals shall be mounted on anti-vibra- 
tion pad. 

16 ENCLOSURES 

16.1 All electrical equipments shall be properly 
enclosed or protected to suit the environmental 



conditions and to prevent inadvertent or accidental 
contact. 

16.2 All enclosures shall preferably be of cast iron 
oi cast aluminium. Wherever sheet steel has to be 
used, the thickness of the metal shall not be less 
than 2 mm where enclosures are housed inside a 
covered area. All exposed outside panels shall have 
a metal thickness of not less than 3.15 mm. 

16.2.1 All joints in enclosures shall be made dust- 
proof by gaskets of high quality non-deteriorating 
rubber or other suitable material. 

16.3 Bare conductors, if used as bus wires, shall be 
located so as to prevent any form of accident con- 
tact. The execution shall ensure complete safety 
and wherever necessary, metallic screens shall be 
employed. 

16.4 Flame proof/increased safety enclosures shall 
be provided for electrical equipments wherever 
required. 

16.5 Wherever it is necessary for an electrical 
equipment to be adjusted or inspected while in 
service, necessary clearances from live parts shall be 
provided to ensure safety in operation. 

16.6 All enclosures of electrical equipments shall 
be given two coats of anti-corrosive epoxy paint 
over two coats of primer suitable for epoxy.

16.7 Various control gears like conductors, relays, 
control transformers, etc, shall be housed in dust- 
proof, damp-proof and vermin-proof protective 
panels having doors interlocked with the supply 
control switches. Space heaters shall be provided 
for the panels. 

17 POWER SUPPLY ARRANGEMENTS 

17.1 Power supply shall be controlled by circuit 
breaker from which power shall be taken to the 
bucket wheel reclaimer through suitable collector 
systems such as cable reeling drums, etc. 

17j2 It shall be possible to trip the shore-based 
circuit breaker from the machinery house of the 
reclaimer. 

17.3 Circuit breaker to isolate the main supply to 
the reclaimer shall be provided. It shall be possible 
to switch off this breaker from the operator's cabin. 

17.4 A plug and socket arrangement shall be 
provided to connect up the reclaimer's current 
collecting arrangements (power and control). 

17.4.1 In case of plug and socket arrangements, 
power socket shall be interlocked so that the plug 
can be withdrawn only when the circuit breaker/ 
isolating switch is 'OFF'. 

17.5 External control links shall be taken out of the 
reclaimer using a current collection system similar 
to that for power. This system may carry the 
communication links also. 

18 POWER TRANSFORMERS 

Power transformers if provided shall be dry type. 
They shall have overload, voltage and temperature 
protection. 

19 MOTORS 

19.1 To select a motor, the following shall be 
established: 

a) The maximum torque required to produce 
motioi n the most unfavourable case. 

b) That t shall allow the reclaimer to perform 
its normal duty without abnormal wear. 

19.2 AC motors shall be of totally enclosed, fan 
cooled, weather protected, hose-proof design (IP- 
55 degree of protection) with atleast class B insula- 
tion. 

19.2.1 AC motors shall have thermistor, over- 
load and short circuit protection. 

19.3 DC motors shall have atleast IP-23 enclosure 
for indoor duty and IP-55 enclosure for outdoor 
dutv. 



19.4 The motors shall have atleast the following 
cyclic duty factors: 

Bucket wheel 50 percent 

Boom conveyor 40 percent 

Travel 40 percent 

Slewing 40 percent 

19.4.1 Motors shall have built in thermistors with 
alarm and shall have space heating elements either 
winding heating or space heater elements as 
specified. 

19.5 Thyristor drives if provided shall have cur- 
rent limitation, speed limitation, overload 
protection, short circuit protection and fuse 
protection and preferably rated for Class 4 duty, 

20 RESISTORS 

Resistors if provided shall be intermittent rated for 
Class 4 duty and shall be suitable for the climatic 
conditions and the corrosive atmosphere described 
elsewhere. Stainless steel resistance grids are 
preferred. 

21 CABLES 

21.1 All power cables shall be PVC insulated, 
armoured and PVC sheathed flexible type 
preferably with copper conductors. However, 
trailing cables shall have copper conductors only. 
All control cables shall be 2.5 mm copper. 

21.2 Power and control cables shall be protected 
with HRC fuses/circuit breakers. 

21.3 The sizes of the cables shall be so chosen that 
they are rated to carry the full load current con- 
tinuously after allowing for necessary derating fac- 
tors for the conditions of installation, and also so 
that the voltage drop in cables shall not exceed 
3 percent during running. 

21.4 All cables shall be neatly clamped at suitable 
intervals. 

21.5 All cables shall be terminated by suitable 
cable glands. 

21.6 Metallic conduits shall be provided wherever 
necessary for the protection of the cables. 

21.7 Power and control cables to the operator's 
cabin shall be provided with a loop to take care 
of movement of cabin during luff operations. 

21.8 Electrical circuits may be printed/painted/ 
pasted at the inside of control panel covers.

shoes rubbing on the rails. In addition, the

23 LIGHTING 

23.1 The reclaimer shall be provided with suitable

23.2 Flood lights shall be provided at the follow-

HRC fuses and shall be controlled by one main 
switch fuse in addition to separate circuit control 
switches. 

23.4 Battery operated emergency lights shall be 
provided at strategic locations. These shall be the 
automatic type. The light shall automatically 
come 'ON' when normal power supply fails and 
shall go 'OFF' automatically when normal supply 
comes back. The unit shall incorporate a trickle 
charger for keeping the battery on charge. The 
battery shall be rated for atleast 30 minutes 
operation.

Statutory Authority for which separate battery 
units with chargers shall be provided.

shall be provided. 

24 CONTROLS 

A safe low voltage shall be employed for the 
controls. This shall be from the secondary wind- 
ing of an isolating transformer. One terminal of 
this supply shall be earthed and the contactor and 
the relay coils shall be connected to this terminal. 



25 INTERLOCKS 

25.1 Necesary interlocks for the safe and smooth 
operation of the reclaimer shall be provided. 

25.2 'Forward' and 'Reverse' contactors shall be 
interlocked electrically and preferably mechanical- 
ly as well. 

26 PROTECTION 

26.1 In case of wind speed beyond preset level, 
an audio-visual alarm shall be given in operator's 
cabin to enable the operator to stop the opera- 
tion of the machine. If the operation is not 
stopped , automaticcut-off of power supply shall 
be effective beyond a preset higher wind speed. 

26.2 Shunt and series limit switches shall be 
provided for all motions. The shunt switch shall 
operate first and if the motion fails to stop, series 
limit switch shall act. Additional limit switches 
shall be provided for long travel, slewing, etc. 

26.2.1 Instead of shunt and series limit switches, 
mechanical type end-limit switches may be 
provided. The pre-limit switch shall act first to 
trip the motor and apply mechanical bjakes. On 
reaching the end-limit switch, the breaker on the 
secondary side of the transformer shall get 
tripped. 

26.3 Phase failure relay shall be provided to trip 
the main circuit breaker. 

26.4 A warning audio-alarm shall be given auto- 
matically during long travel operation. 

27 AIR CONDITIONING 

In the interest of long life and reliable operation, 
electronic components and other components 
sensitive to temperature, humidity and dust, shall 
preferably be located in air conditioned space(s). 

28 COMMUNICATION FACILITIES 

Inter-communication facility within structure is 
recommended. 

29 AUTOMATION 

Where considered essential, automation may be 
provided to reduce physical and psychological 
stress of operator. Provision shall be kept for over- 
riding automation.
