1 SCOPE 

This standard specifies important material charac- 
teristics for woven fabrics for awnings and camping 
tents. To meet the needs arising from different 
climatic conditions, different national habits in 
tent usage or different durability-expectations of 
the customer, the material requirements are split 
into two levels: A and B. Level A requirements 
apply to awnings and camping tent fabrics intended 
for severe and/or long term use; level B require- 
ments are lower than level A and apply to fabrics 
intended for less severe use. 

NOTE — As far as the division and subdivision in different 
lent-categories and types is concerned this standard follows 
the classification as given below: 

a) Awnings 

Residential awning — Type R 
Touring awning — Type T 

Snow awning use — Type SN 

b) Camping tents 

— Sleeping tent (Type S); 

1) Class 1 -Light weight tent 

2) Class St - Standard weight tent 

— Touring tent (Type T) 

— Residential tent (Type R)

3 TERMINOLOGY 

3,1 In addition to definitions given in IS 12991/ 
ISO 7152 following definitions shall apply. 

3.1.1 Inner Fabric 

Fabric for awning and camping tents which is not 
directly exposed to weather during practical (that is 
protected by other fabric). 



3.1.2 Outer Fabric 

Fabric for awning and camping tents which is 
directly exposed to weather during practical use.

4.8 Resistance Against Cold Crack 

Cracks of grade A [see 9.1 of IS 7016 (Part 10)] are 
permitted in all coated fabrics when tested as per 
IS 7016 (Part 10). The test temperature shall be 
-20^C for snow tent materials and -lO^C for 
materials of all other types. 

4.9 Dimensional Stability 

Dimensional change shall not be more than ±3 
percent when tested in accordance with IS 2977 
using a cycle of 2 h.

4.10 Colour Fastness 

The fabrics shall meet the following colour fastness 
ratings when tested in accordance with relevant 
Indian Standards mentioned against each require- 
ment: 

a) Outer fabric of all types of awnings and 
camping tents 

— fastness to light: 6 (IS 2454) 

— fastness to weathering:4-5 (IS 6152) 

— fastness to water: 4 (IS 767) 

— fastness to wet rubbing: 3 (IS 766) 

b) Inner fabrics 

— fastness to water:4 (IS 767) 

— fastness to wet rubbing: 3 (IS 766) 

4.11 Breaking Strength of Inner Fabrics 

Inner tent fabrics for all types of tents shall have a 
minimum breaking strength of 30 daN for warp and 
weft when tested in accordance with IS 7016 

(Part 2). 

4.12 Weatherability 

All outer fabrics (roof, walls, etc) shall provide a 
minimum resistance to natural sunlight regardless 
to the type of tent or awning concerned. This 
requirement is deemed to be met, if after artificial 
weathering in accordance with IS 6152 and applying 
the test parameters as specified in Table 8, the 
breaking strength and the resistance to penetration 
by water is not more than 30 percent below the 
minimum value applicable to the type and part of 
the tent specified in Table 1 to 7. 

4.13 Flammability (Option) 

This standard does not specify a general minimum 
requirement concerning flame retardation of 
fabrics for awnings and camping tents. If the fabric 
of an awning and camping tent is claimed to have a 



flame retardant performance, it shall not ignite 
when tested in accordance with IS 11871 and 
exposed to a test flame for 10 s.

information on the label attached: 

a) Brief nomenclature, 

b) Performance of the fabric, that is its clas- 
sification to level A or B, 

c) Fabrics complying with the requirements 
according to clause 4.13 may be marked to 
have flame retardant performance, 

d) Manufacturer's name/initials or recognized 
trade mark, and 

e) Month and year of manufacture.

to a buyer against one despatch note shall con-

manufacturers should follow the methods given in 
IS 397 (Part 1) and IS 397 (Part 2).
