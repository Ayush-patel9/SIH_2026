0.    FOREWORD 

0.1  This  Indian  Standard  was  adopted  by  the  Bureau  of  Indian 
Standards  on  3  August  1987,  after  the  draft  finalized  by  the  Cement  arid 
Concrete  Sectional  Committee  had  been  approved  by  the  Civil 
Engineering  Division  Council. 

0.2  In  recent  years  there  has  been  a  growing  awareness  that  exposure  to 
asbestos  dust  can  have  harmful  effect  on  the  health  of  workers.  Accor- 
dingly, a  number  of  standards  giving  technical  guidance  on  how  the 
risk  of  exposure  to  asbestos  dust  can  be  prevented,  controlled  or  mini- 
mized, have  been  taken  up  for  formulation.  It  has  also  been  felt  necess- 
ary that  some  pictorial  warning  signs  and  precautionary  notices  shall  be 
used  at  workplaces  involving  asbestos  and  asbestos  products,  packages 
containing  asbestos  and  asbestos  products  and  also  on  the  asbestos  pro- 
ducts, in  order  to  caution  the  workers  and  users  about  the  potential 
health  hazards  of  asbestos.  Keeping  this  in  view,  this  standard  regard- 
ing pictorial  warning  signs  and  precautionary  notices  has  been  formulated 
in  two  parts  as  follows: 

a)  Recommendations  for  pictorial  warning  signs  and  precautionary 
notices  for  asbestos  and  products  containing  asbestos:  Part  1 
Workplaces 

b)  Recommendations  for  pictorial  warning  signs  and  precautionary 
notices  for  asbestos  and  products  containing  asbestos:  Part  2 
Asbestos  and  its  products 

0.3  In  the  formulation  of  this  standard,  due  weightage  has  been  given 
to  international  co-ordination  among  the  standards  and  practices 
prevailing  in  different  countries,  in  addition  to  relating  it  to  the  practices 
in  the  field  in  this  country.

1.  SCOPE 

1.1  This  standard  deals  with  pictorial  warning  signs  and  precautionary 
notices  recommended  for  use  with  asbestos  and  products  containing 
asbestos. 

2.  OBJECT 

2.1  The  object  of  this  standard  is  to  caution  those  engaged  in  handling 
of  asbestos  and  products  containing  asbestos  so  as  to  enable  them  to  take 
necessary  precautionary  measures  against  the  risks  from  exposure  to 
asbestos  dust. 

3.  LABELLING 

3.1  All  asbestos  packages,  products  containing  asbestos  and  the  packages 
of  such  products,  excepting  those  mentioned  in  3.2,  shall  bear  a  pictorial 
warning  sign  and  precautionary  notice  as  shown  in  Fig.  1. 

3«2  Labelling  of  products  containing  asbestos  in  accordance  with  3.1 
may  be  dispensed  with  or  modified,  subject  to  the  conditions  given  in  3.2.1 
to  3.2.3.2. 

3.2.1  Labelling  on  the  product  may  be  dispensed  with  if  smallness  of 
size  of  the  products  makes  it  impossible  for  the  product  to  be  labelled. 
Such  products  shall  be  supplied  in  a  package  which  shall  be  duly 
labelled  as  given  in  3.1. 

3.2.2  Labelling  of  compressed  asbestos  fibre  jointings  and  beater 
addition  jointings  in  which  asbestos  is  firmly  encapsulated  with  a  strong 
bonding  agent  and  will  not  release  asbestos  dust  during  normal  handling 
and  use,  may  be  dispensed  with. 

3.2.3  In  case  of  unpacked  articles,  for  example,  asbestos  cement  sheet 
and  pipe  where  it  is  not  practicable  to  mark  the  pictorical  warning  sign 
and  precautionary  notice  as  given  in  3.1,  the  procedure  given  in  3.2.3.1 
and  3.2.3.2  shall  be  adopted. 

3.2.3.1  The  symbol  ^a'  as  portrayed  in  Fig.  1  ( except  the 
precautionary  notice  )  shall  be  printed/stamped  on  the  article  very 
conspicuously. 

3.2.3.2  Pictorial  warning  sign  and  precautionary  notice  as  given 
in  3.1  shall  be  printed  on  literatures  given  to  a  purchaser, 

3*3  Appropriate   safety  rules  sheet  shall  be  provided  along  with  asbestos 
and  products  containing  asbestos.
